Top 5 books all time for me. Scott Lynch goes stupid hard for no reason. Following the story of a likable thief is so much more interesting than normal good guys. Jean is the best friend anyone could hope for. Almost cried at certain parts and forced myself to read slow most of the time because I wanted to savor the book. Reading this and Name of the Wind at the same time was like my own little slice of heaven. What a good book to be number 50. This will be at the top of the tier list no doubt.
