2023 | Score: 8

I listened to 15 hours of this in a single day. Not because it was good but because I had already planned to. Time-loop progression fantasy, which is a fun concept. The book was not paced at all how I thought it would, but it was well done. There were definitely some slower patches, but overall it had satisfying progression and an interesting story line. Overall a fun read, good progression.

Read on Royal Road. The writing gets better even throughout the course of this first book, but still not great. For an online read though it is better than 95% of the other stuff you will find in terms of grammar and dialogue. The real winner is the plot is just super entertaining and progression fantasy with a time loop works very very well. Super fun to read but nothing super serious.
