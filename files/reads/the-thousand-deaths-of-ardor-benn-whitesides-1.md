An awesome combination of Lies of Locke Lamora, Mistborn: The Final Empire, and Dragons. Set in a world reminiscent of the Wizard of Earthsea, the author clearly pulled from many different inspirations and then made them into a fantastic story of his own. I really, really enjoyed this book and am excited to read the rest of the series.
