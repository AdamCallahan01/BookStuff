So much better than the last book I almost refuse to believe they are from the same series. By far my favorite track on the project. Characters finally started actually evolving and growing, the action was frequent and well written, and it both separated and then brought back together multiple plot lines without feeling forced. The last 200 pages I read in like an hour I was enjoying it so much. A great conclusion to an overall not great series in my opinion, maybe a little to much “everythig worked out perfectly” considering how dark parts of the books are but still good.
