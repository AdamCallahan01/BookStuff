This audiobook was actually read by the author which is pretty cool, I also like books that follow thieves. Cool world and funny accent. People don’t always mention how utterly important the narrator is for an audiobook. Like the best book with a bad narrator can be completely ruined but I think what happens more often is a phenomonal voice actor can take an average book and make it really good. But oh my gosh I hate it when the mispronounce words. Like one time in a book i forget they kept pronouncing “Dais” like “Dye-us” and I hated it. It may be technically correct but it is not how I say it.
