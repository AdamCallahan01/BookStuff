I’m not sure I’m going to keep reading the series. I still like the plot focused on the greater universe and Carl trying to change that, but I am getting more and more bored with the nonsense going on in the dungeon. It doesn’t feel like there are any real stakes yet (in terms of people we care about dying), although I’m sure that will change in later books. Part of me is curious enough about how the story ends (and I have already read 6 books that keep getting longer) to want to finish, but I just don’t really enjoy large chunks of the story anymore. I get bored of the same thing with slightly different enemies and characters over and over again and Carl getting angry and Donut saying something funny. We’ll if I read any more books.
