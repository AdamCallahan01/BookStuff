I read this a decade ago and don’t remember much about it. This is a super cool, action packed story with an interesting plot but fairly boring characters and plain writing. Some stuff (like the powder addiction) is shoved down your throat instead of just letting the reader realize what is happening, plus there basically aren’t any female characters (Nila does nothing). The whole thing when Taniel realizes how old Ka-Poel is is really really weird, did not like that. I also wish we got some more explanations of how the non-powder magic works, but maybe that will come later. But the plot was still fun enough to make me want to continue with the series.
