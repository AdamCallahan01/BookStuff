My 500th tracked book! Pretty disappointing. It had a lot of aspects I found interesting or enjoyable, but overall just became boring. I loved the breakdown of religion and the history of Sumer, but it was counterbalanced with fairly dull characters and a weird, funky plot. On top of that the oversexualization of a 15 year old girl was not my favorite thing in the world. Partly I think I have just been doing too much sci-fi this year and am burnt out a little, but nothing in this book would make me want to read it again or continue if it was a series. The ending was strange and abrupt, and I can’t decide if naming the main character Protagonist is funny or stupid. The best part outside of the religious theorizing is probably the satire of an overly corporate future which seems to slowly be coming true.
