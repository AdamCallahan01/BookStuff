Pretty Good. I didn’t enjoy the first or last 15% very much but the middle section was solid. The audiobook narrator took some getting used to but after a while I started to like it. Heavily Roman inspired so parts reminded me of Will of the Many and Red Rising, but still felt somewhat unique and original which is impressive in such a saturated genre mix. I plan to continue the series, curious to see how it develops after the ending to this book.
