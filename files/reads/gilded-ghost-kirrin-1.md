Best book in the series so far, I really liked the development of House, getting to see an AI not achieve humanity, but simply fake it is a fun concept. I thought this was the end of a trilogy but there is still more books so he needs to keep writing. Honestly I am surprised this is actually starting to feel like a well written series, definitely the best LitRPG I’ve read. If the action/battle scenes get better that would be nice.
