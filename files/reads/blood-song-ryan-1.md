A great book that left me unsure how the series will continue. Could be a standalone but left the world open for more growth. This had some great worldbuilding throughout, without ever dumping information at you. It’s biggest flaw would probably be Veilan being too perfect. His connection to the Blood Song aids him in all things, but he never once makes a mistake in the book. The drama and conflict comes from watching those around him and how he changes the lives of others. I will continue the series at some point but didn’t end on a cliffhanger that makes me want to dive into the next one.
