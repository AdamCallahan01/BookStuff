Had everything great about the first book but I found the plot way more engaging. Malta remains one of the best characters. The section with Serilla was brutal. Certain sections remained very slow, but this book felt a lot more balanced where between Kennit, Paragon, or Reyn/Malta something was happening to keep the plot advancing. This story takes place in a time with a lot of change happening, and my opinion keeps getting changed on if it is positive or negative. I am excited and worried for the last book in the trilogy.
