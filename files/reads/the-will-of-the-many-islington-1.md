Best book I will read this year. Started off strong as a setup for a university fantasy book, but hit and changed so many classic themes and tropes. The last 20% is a huge tone shift and so amazing. Turns into a psychological thriller during the final few chapters, then the epilogue was the most insane twist ever. Normally you can kind of feel big plot changes coming but this caught me completely by surprise and I loved it.
