I can’t remember what I scored this last time, but I think I enjoyed it more on the reread. The language can still feel a bit forced at times, especially with certain characters (Talon), but the story itself outshines any writing decisions made. I am also very excited to read the trilogy, since it was not complete the last time I read the book. I know some people don’t like gentlemen bastards because of the language and I imagine those people would very much dislike this book, but it just doesn’t seem important when the actual plot is so intriguing. I am assuming the second book will have less of the 2 stories/times to jump between and just follow one timeline of the story.
