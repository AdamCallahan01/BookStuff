This was painful to read. Really bad. Just retold a couple of ancient myths, primarily Theseus and the Minotaur, from Ariadne’s perspective. I understand the concept of promoting the female voice but it was honestly just poor writing, bad character work, did not pass the Bechtel test, and tried to use big words whenever possible even if they were used incorrectly.
