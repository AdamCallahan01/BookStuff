Fantastic Series, I really hope he finishes Thorn at some point before I die. The second book is awesome but is definitely the weakest in the series in my opinion. I love the time cuts between past and present in the first and third books, learning about Locke’s upbringing and past trials. I honestly can’t say whether I like this book or LoLL more, but both are fantastic 10/10 reads. This is one of my favorite series of all time, even without it being finished. Also the epilogue oh my goodness gracious.
