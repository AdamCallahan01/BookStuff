I’m not really sure what score to give this one. Parts of it were super engaging and we learned more about the universe, but some sections were also super slow and felt like nothing got added. Throughout the first half Elvi was the only character really keeping me engaged but the back half started to pick up and the last 200 pages were really exciting. The space combat felt the most real and understandable of any in the series so far, which was great. Going to go right into the final book, excited to see what happens.
