I’m not the target audience but still thought this book just didn’t have anything going for it. Read more like a bad romance without any real plot. Our main character is an alcoholic halfling who can’t make a decision and is super inconsistent. Can’t really even compare to legends & lattes since it was just so much worse. It is also the third book in a series (Although they seem to be entirely unconnected) so maybe I would have enjoyed it more if I read the first two books. I also don’t know how this business makes any profit considering how much of their own wine they drink or just give away, it certainly seems like a bad investment.
