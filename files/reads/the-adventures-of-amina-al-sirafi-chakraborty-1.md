Nothing in particular stands out in either a good or bad way. Overall just a mostly uninteresting story with a few cool ideas and characters. The stakes never really felt that high, despite the supposed dangers and threats, and the villain was like a comical disney bad guy just designed to have his power stolen and be killed. I don’t think I am the target audience for this book, but probably the last book I read by her. There was also only one instance in the entire book where Amina and her crew felt even vaguely “pirate-y”, which is disappointing for a book about pirates. Way too much time was spent trying to describe them all as actually good people who just do pirating in a nice way on the side.
