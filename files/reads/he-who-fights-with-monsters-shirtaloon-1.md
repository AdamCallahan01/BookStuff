Another LitRPG novel, but this one had some of the best reviews for any book in the genre besides Ready Player One so I was interested. It started out pretty rough but the writing got better as it went along. Not the best world building and a horrible magic system but had a lot of action so it stayed interesting. My biggest qualm is the beginning, I don’t want to spoil it but the beginning sucks.
2026: I will be rereading this sometime. Don't trust me from 4 years ago.
