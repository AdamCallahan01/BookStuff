I read this as a part of the Theft of Swords, but due to series numbering broke it up into the two smaller books. A fun, classic fantasy story. Nothing done especially well but no glaring weaknesses. The dialogue can be a bit rough at times and some of the character arcs feel rushed, but that feels ok within the world. It is a fast-paced, big stakes but small encounters adventure. I would recommend this is a great introductory book to what fantasy can feel like, an adventure story with a bit of elves and magic tossed in. Royce and Hadrian are both cleverer and better than the people around them, which can bother a lot of people but if you can ignore that and just enjoy the story and world this is a great book.
