There were points I almost stopped reading this. Incredibly brutal sections throughout that genuinely made it hard to continue. Despite that the story Butler tells is so vivid and real that by the end I discovered I loved the book. A story featuring aliens but really delves into the human condition. The anger you feel at almost every character in this story is astounding. I think this book is best to go into with little background on, but make sure you are ready for a hard, violent book at times.
