I am very excited to watch the movie. This was a pretty quick read and definitely intended for a younger audience, but I enjoyed it. I always love stuff that takes classic fairy tale tropes and plays with them. The idea that since Sophie is the oldest she must be boring and the youngest sister is the one destined to have adventure is fun. We are watching the movie tomorrow night. Also doors are powerful.
