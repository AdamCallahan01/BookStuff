Many of my same thoughts for the first book. I still think the world and general concept are great, but honestly the character work got worse in this one. I really enjoyed getting Jardir’s backstory, so the first third or so of the book was great, but certain parts with Arlen the edgelord, Renna, and everything that happened in Miln were almost unbearable. Plus, the weird sex stuff did continue unfortunately. This book kind of cemented that the negatives for the series won’t get fixed, but I got them all on audible on sale because I thought I would like them so I will try to finish out the series while playing minecraft or something.
