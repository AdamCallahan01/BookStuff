This is a hard one for me to give a score to. The book has strong militaristic and political themes throughout, and the story itself doesn’t have a ton of content outside of Heinlein expressing those themes. Johnny is one of the most bland narrators ever, which I assume is intentional. The book explicitly calls out many practices of 20th century democracy and military and Heinlein seems to be very critical of them. In this world, only those who serve are citizens with the right to vote. Overall, a short, interesting read, without a ton of “book content” to enjoy.
