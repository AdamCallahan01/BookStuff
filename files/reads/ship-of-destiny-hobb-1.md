Every book in the Realm of the Elderlings makes me love the world more and more. I don’t know right now if this one or Assassin’s Quest is better, but both are amazing. The entire trilogy felt like every chapter was better than the last, and this brought it all together with a phenomenal climax. The characters are so vivid and real, and I wasn’t sure how their relationships would resolve even up to the last few pages. Robin Hobb has the best character work out there, and I am so excited to continue the series. I highly recommend reading this book. Also was a fan of the quote from Althea to Live life, don’t hoard it for an unseen tomorrow.
