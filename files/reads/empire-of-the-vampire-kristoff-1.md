A phenomenal book. Something about it just wasn’t perfect for me but I still really enjoyed it because the writing and world and characters are amazing. Probably the only good retelling and use of vampires that are super strong and fast I have read. I was very glad we got some reveals before the end of the book but there is still a lot I want to find out, will have to finish the series at some point.
