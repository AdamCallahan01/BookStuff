Recommended to me by a friend a while ago. I did not listen to the podcasts, but I did not know they existed until after I read it so the book can be enjoyed without them, but I imagine it would enhance it a lot to listen to them beforehand. The first half was awesome, felt like a modern, adult ready player one and I was super hooked. The back half got very science fiction and strange, which was still good but much less my style. The overall mystery and puzzles remain consistent throughout the story however and I bet a lot of people would love this. I also like books set in Seattle. The audiobook was also really well done.
