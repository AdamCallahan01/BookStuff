Reminded me of an adult version of the hunger games. A cool dystopian setting, with a bit of mystery as to how we reached this point and what is going on behind the scenes. Went into this knowing genuinely nothing and was pleasantly surprised as it went along. The end felt a bit anticlimactic, and didn’t set up for future books as well as I expected. Looking at the blurbs for the rest of the series I don’t think I will read the books but do want to watch the TV show.
