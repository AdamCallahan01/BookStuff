Coming back to The Wandering Inn after taking a break for the first time in almost two years exposed some of the writing flaws I had become accustomed to. I also think this was just generally a weaker entry in the series overall. Hard to say much without spoilers so deep into a series but there was a major event that happened and a lot of the reactions to it felt uncharacteristic. Some of the power levels between characters also feel a bit odd at points. Still had a lot to enjoy, and the climaxes were as broad and epic as always, but this is a low point in the series for me.
