I would say this is an overly average book in pretty much every aspect, with a few interesting ideas but not enough to keep me interested. Definitely more of a YA book than I thought going into it, set in an achingly average fantasy world. The writing was fine (arms akimbo excluded) and the dialogue a lot better than most books I score this low, but it really just didn’t have anything to hook me as a reader. The characters were probably the best written part but still were only slightly above the mean. I think this is fine for a YA series but there are a lot of better books doing classic fantasy tropes a lot better. I will say it did a great job sticking to what it set out to do by keeping Conrad as primarily a “squire”, not letting him suddenly become some god of the sword which happens fairly often.
