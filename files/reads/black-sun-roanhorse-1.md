Short book, got a lot better as it went on. I would recommend not doing the audiobook for this one, took a lot out of the story. The writing is solid, but just a pretty bland story for most of the first half but then shoves a ton of action in the back half. It was enough to make me want to finish the series someday, but better pacing could have made this a way better book. I also went on a nice long walk when I was finishing it and that always helps.
