A great finish to what will probably be one of my top series of the year (even though technically I started the first book last year). I really love how the POV characters from the previous book in the series become mentors/leaders in the next book. It reminds me of The Giver series in that regard. This was my favorite book out of the trilogy, and it was cool to see Yarvi complete his character arc.
