Big nostalgia book for me. Not incredible on a reread, but I love fairy tale retellings and think this one still does a great job in that regard. Tying in the history of the real life Alice Liddell is something I did not know about until I researched it just now. I also know the trivia questions of Lewis Carroll’s real name and what a milliner is. Very fast paced, probably too much so, but a good book for middle school age. Arguably the softest magic system of all time just based on the nature of imagination which is interesting. I can’t remember what happens in the second and third book since there doesn’t really seem to be any plot left after the first one, so I will probably finish the series.
