I have a hard time deciding how I feel about this book. It is short, fast-paced, but still establishes some notable characters and explains the world, but also just seemed kind of samey and like nothing really happened despite stuff going on. I don’t recommend audio for this one, and if I continue the series I will read it physically. Didn’t do anything original but instead took a bunch of fairly common fantasy ideas and turned them into a somewhat interesting story.
