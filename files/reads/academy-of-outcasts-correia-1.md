A solid 4. The start was especially bad but it picked up a bit towards the back. The beginning made me think I was about to read a Red Rising rip-off. The magic system is honestly just not very interesting, I have no real desire to see what happens as the characters get stronger. The world has a couple interesting pieces with the different elemental realms and a time based connection mechanic but nothing that unique there either. Probably won’t read any more of this guy's books since I have not liked either of the ones I tried and they were both very different.
