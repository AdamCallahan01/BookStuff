Such a good book. Struck the perfect balance of sci-fi world, fantasy elements, mystery plot, with political intrigue touching everything. I was excited and thinking about this often during the day waiting to read it. Mycroft is such an interesting character to have as our narrator, and the framing perspective definitely adds questions about how we are learning about these events. I am very excited to continue the series, this is an awesome  book.
