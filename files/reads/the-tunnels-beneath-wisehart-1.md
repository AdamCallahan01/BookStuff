The series keeps getting better every book. Adarra is the first one to stop being such a mary sue, which is a nice change. I had initially thought this was the last book but there is clearly at least one more, I would guess probably 2 or 3 even. It was interesting to have a book without following the central Aramoor plot at all, no Dakaran or Barthol or anyone else in the capital.
