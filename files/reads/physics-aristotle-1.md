An interesting read. A lot of Aristotle’s claims feel like common sense when read from a modern viewpoint, however he was clearly exploring ideas and concepts that not many had tackled before. And despite having some points that are laughably false, in general I think he made good solid arguments and approached his ideas in an appropriately scientific manner.
