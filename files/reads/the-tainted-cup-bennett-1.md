A really cool, unique world where we follow the perspective of a protagonist who is pretty much a “Watson” witnessing a smart detective deduce stuff. The body mutations are interesting, the broader world in danger of leviathans is interesting, the mystery is ok. The surrounding world does the heavy lifting for this which makes me want to continue deeper into the series. Audiobook was not my favorite narration ever.
