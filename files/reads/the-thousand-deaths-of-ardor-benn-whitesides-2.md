Definitely has a lot of flaws (with general writing, character work, pace, world economy / politics, etc.), but in spite of all that the plot and magic and cool factor are just so awesome that they still make it an incredible book. I still think it is one of the most unique modern takes on dragons in the genre, and even if they aren’t on a Locke / Jean level, Ard and Raek are a great duo, if a bit goofier. Even though it can feel heavy-handed at times I like that the author leans into the silliness of parts of the story. Also narrating your own book and doing a good job is impressive. I genuinely have no idea where the rest of the series will go but I own the entire trilogy and am very excited to continue. Plus I got to listen to the first chunk of it while hiking the dolomites so that was pretty awesome.
