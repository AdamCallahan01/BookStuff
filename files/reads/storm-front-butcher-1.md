The first Dresden Files book, which I have been wanting to read for a long time. I think I hyped it up a little too much in my head since I was disappointed by it, even though it was still good. A wizard set in a noir detective role is a super clever idea and Jim Butcher manages to make it feel natural. I definitely still want to finish the series I think I’m just not the biggest detective novel person.
