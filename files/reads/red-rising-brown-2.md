Still reads like a YA book, but still very enjoyable. Incredibly fast paced, a good amount of exposition and world building, and an easy premise. Very reminiscent of the Hunger Games but that is not a bad thing. I am hoping the other books expand more. I still don’t like when dystopian books just take normal words and alter them slightly even though it kind of makes sense for a world set in the future.
