Classic fantasy story with just a bit too much plot armor. I did think it was a cool way to use dragons and the fact that they are big enough to carry far more than a single rider, that aspect was really cool. I also liked the behind the scenes politics happening, but the front face of the story was just pretty bland. There were enough elements I liked that I may finish it someday, but I hope in the future books it can cut down the random nothingness and keep advancing the plot. This sort of just feels like worse Bound and the Broken, so for most people I recommend just reading that.
