A return to form from the last book. Almost feels like the start of a new trilogy, where we had books 1-6 together, and now we have the final ones in a group. I really enjoyed the Laconia focused chapters, Winston Duarte is a great character. I hope we get more POV’s from him or people close to him. This one probably would have been more enjoyable if I had taken a break after the last Expanse book, they need buffer stuff between them or they start to drag on. I am hopeful the last two books will be able to pull off a good conclusion.
