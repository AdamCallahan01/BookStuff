Three may even be generous. The writing was slightly better than some web serial litRPG, but there were dumb mistakes like just calling a character the wrong name. Not technically a litRPG, just progression, but the author wrote like it was a video game, including stuff like enemies having set attack patterns, being able to teleport to differently themed areas, boss monsters, etc. The world, characters, and most importantly magic system just were not interesting, which is the most important part of a progression book. But the Seahawks won the superbowl so everything is ok.
