Overall, this series is an incredibly disappointing take on what was a fun and inventive concept. Each book got worse and worse while getting longer and longer, and all of the added length was just filler where nothing really happens or develops. The “climax” to the final book probably could have been guessed by a child reading the first one, and also happened in the last 4% of the book?? Genuinely everything got shoved in right at the end. Feels like the last book and the first 80% of this one just got ignored and the ending had to be shoved in magically. Also, one last gripe, the timeline makes no sense. It has only been like a year since the end of the first book. The first book is a decent read on its own but I would not recommend continuing with the series after that.
