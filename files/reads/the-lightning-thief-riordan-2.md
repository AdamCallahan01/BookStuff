Rick Riordan and Suzanne Collins are probably the two most foundational authors for me growing up. Listening to Gregor the Overland on books on tape and reading all the new Percy Jackson books as they came out are part of why I love reading. These books definitely read better a kid, but despite the writing and character relationship being pretty juvenile, the story itself remains interesting and the weaving of mythology into the world will always be fun for me to read. A couple big qualms I have are the numbers in camp Half-Blood never seem to add up. He says about 100 campers, but then looking at the individual cabin numbers that does not make sense. Another thing is why do people never just explain the situation to young protagonists. They are always faced with people withholding information for no reason.
