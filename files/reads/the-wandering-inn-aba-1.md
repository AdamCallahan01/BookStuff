I finished this book at 11:58 last night, so just barely within the 2 week barrier. This is a web serial so you wouldn’t expect the best writing, but the author did go back and redo the first book (this version) a few months ago so it is better than it could have been. Despite how bad the writing was in certain places and the weird grammar and inconsistencies, I loved this. It was fun, easy to read for most of it, then at certain sections, it got incredibly emotional and made you connect deeply with the characters. And it is still coming out and the books are super long so I am very excited to continue.
