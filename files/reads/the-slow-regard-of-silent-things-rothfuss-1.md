I thought this was an amazing insight into Patrick Rothfuss’ world. It was honestly really heartbreaking and sad to see the fragility of Auri’s mind and how distressed she can get, but there is a beauty to knowing the way of things that somehow I connected with a lot. I think all broken people should read this and reflect.
