Hard to say much about this one without spoilers for it and the first two books, but even though part of me expected the ending to happen the way it did, I kept expecting that we were being intentionally misled. The elements of mystery incorporated into this were perfect, where once you figure something out you can see more and more clues pointing to it. I really enjoyed this trilogy, and the ending explains a few of the more questionable writing decisions. If you won’t be offended by very crude language and explicit scenes and childish jokes, this is a fantastic story. Probably the biggest example of an unreliable narrator I have ever read.
