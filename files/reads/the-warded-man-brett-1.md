The story, worldbuilding, and plot were all incredible for most of the book, but it got heavily dragged down by the super weird sex stuff brought up with various characters. Most of the scenes felt almost entirely unnecessary to the plot development, and combined with how some of the women are described is just so so weird, I really did not like it. The only major issue I had with the plot was some of the time jumps we took, with Arlen at the end being funky. I am going to continue the series but I really hope some of the weird stuff gets cut out because I think the world and premise is awesome.
