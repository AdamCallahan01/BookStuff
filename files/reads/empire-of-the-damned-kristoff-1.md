Incredible book, story is even more engaging than the first one. A lot of things to nitpick, the language (while better than the first one, feels much more natural), is still overly vulgar and kind of silly at times. Certain turns of phrase are used far too often, and I would greatly prefer either a steep reduction in the sex scenes or else make them far less explicit, I did not sign up to read Fourth Wing. But none of that takes away from a fantastic adventure, the character relationships, (outside of Lachlan who I am mixed on) are fantastic, Phoebe is a great character, and a bunch of stuff I thought would be in the third book happened in this one, setting up what should be a super exciting conclusion I can not wait to read. I will have to consider reading the Nevernight trilogy by Jay Kristoff since I have enjoyed these so much.
