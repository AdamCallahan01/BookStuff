Read as part of Rise of Empire. The series continues to be quite enjoyable without doing any one thing particularly well. It is easy prose to read, a couple of unlikely friends, fun adventures, with a bit of magic sprinkled in. It reminds me of Skyrim in the best way possible. I am excited to continue and finish the series, as well as check out the other series set in the world of Elan (which I think are all close to being released). I will probably do the other series via audio since I liked doing the first two that way a lot.
