This was so so boring. The magic system felt like it was being made up by the author as the story went along and the power balancing felt terrible because of this. On top of all the characters being incredibly one dimensional as well as dumb people, the concept itself of someone with a ton of knowledge isn’t very fun, and reminded me of all the negatives of The Captain by Will Wight.
