A lot shorter than I thought it was, I think I would have enjoyed if this book was a lot longer. It was impressive the amount of growth by the characters in such a short period, but I think it would have been much more satisfying in a longer form. Robots are cool and trying to interact with humans is hard, so this is a good book.
