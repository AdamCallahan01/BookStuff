Ironbound
Andrew Givler
Ironbound #1

Ironbound Summary

Castor Castus is a patrician from the town of Segesta in the Iron Empire. He is a student in the collegium, and his class is approaching their Sententia, a test to determine the top ranking students. Castor dreams of placing high enough to earn a Cor heart, the mysterious metal things that fall from the sky and grant people the power of Electii, enabling them to bond to an element. On their way home, he and his close friends Marcus and Valentina swear a Votum, a blood oath, that either they all get hearts or none of them do. This is a Roman-esque world and empire, except 400 years ago the “iron lords” killed the gods (Mars, Minerva, etc.).

Censor Atticus arrives to oversee the testing and bring the Cor hearts to Segesta, but he brings unwelcome news. Because it is a drought year for Cor harvesting, the students of Sagesta will also compete against the 50 best from the collegium in nearby Nicodemia for the limited supply. Castor and his classmates are upset, and make a plan to band together in the first part of the test, a large melee. Castor includes Felix, their top student but a plebeian (lower social rank) despite Valentina’s objections. They go into the arena and manage to have the majority of the 50 students that advance be their own classmates, however right at the end of the battle Castor is attacked by Aulus, one of his classmates from Segesta. Aulus knows Castor is ranked ahead of him and wants to improve his chances of getting a heart. Castor is losing the sword fight when Felix throws his sword and hits Aulus, allowing Castor to knock him out and win the fight.

The next part of the challenge is the labyrinthus, a large maze where they must collect three ribbons and escape as fast as possible. After the melee the three friends had added Felix to their Votum, and all get to enter in the top 10. Castor makes good progress, although he has a strange experience retrieving his green ribbon that leaves scars from a dream wolf on his wrist. Him, Felix, and Valentina get cornered by a group from Nicodemia but Marcus saves them and they make their escape. Castor lets all his friends exit before him, and with the two other students who got out fast that makes him sixth for this year. He is ecstatic because that almost guarantees he gets a heart, however is distraught when Atticus announces only 5 hearts will be given out this year due to the drought.

Felix and Marcus come to talk to Castor before the ceremony the following day, and talk about their futures, but Valentina (who has a bit of a romantic thing going on with Castor) does not talk to him. At the ceremony Valentina continues to ignore Castor, hurting him, but everything changes when Felix goes to get his heart and suddenly a golden wolf man appears, causing a large explosion then disappearing. Castor wakes up to find he has been impaled by a bar of iron, but also sees the white veins on his arms meaning he absorbed a Cor heart. He feels himself attune to iron and passes out.

Castor wakes up to find himself and his classmates all gathered up with Atticus raging at him while the town council (Which includes most of his classmates' parents) watches. Atticus tells Castor that he not only got 1 heart, he absorbed the remaining Cor hearts (I think 3 or 4). His veins are only partly copper, marking him as an “immundus”, someone who does not correctly bond to an element with their heart. Censor Atticus knows this will reflect poorly on him, so he calls Castor a thief and sentences him and his male classmates to the Iron Legions of the empire. When the council and their parents start to complain he orders the soldiers with him to kill them all and burn their homes. Castor as forced to watch as Atticus (A silver electii) butchers his family and the town council members. The soldiers force them into cages and they begin the march to Agogia, the Iron City and base of the legions.

Felix is the only one who remains close to Castor as they travel. Marcus and his other classmates seem to blame him. When they arrive at Agogia they are sent to the twelfth legion, typically the place for murderers, thieves, and other convicts. One of the convicts, Anas, immediately dislikes Castor due to his patrician background and already being bound to the iron symbol. They meet their leader, Tribune Gaius Marius, a silver who is for some reason also stuck leading the twelfth. They are all given hearts then brought to a pit of iron cages they must bond and escape from. All members of the iron legion are attuned to the iron symbol. Iron is a deadly symbol for war, but people are scared of them so everyone of them wears and inhibitus, a collar around their neck that tightens if it senses them begin to ascend from copper (first rank) to silver (second rank). Only a select few are selected to ascend to silver, and none are allowed to go to gold, out of fear of making new Iron Lords like the ones that killed the gods 400 years ago.

Castor is the first to escape his cage, branded with the XII symbol of the twelfth, and named Primus for being first. Now we begin a lot of training to become soldiers. As Primus Castor does his best to step up and be a leader, and on one of the first days he takes 5 lashes instead of another man. Macer, who was supposed to be whipped, feels a huge debt to Castor and tries to help him when possible. After being attacked by some drunk members of the legion, Castor and Marcus begin to bond again and get over the grief of their families being murdered. Castor, Marcus, Felix, and some other members of their Segesta class (Clintus) swear a Votum together to avenge their families.

They continue to train with the iron symbol, and Castor seems to be especially adept. Gaius sends the three friends to be trained as Fidelis (faithful), those allowed to ascend to silver. The instruction is led by Gaius’ brother Percy Marius. Castor, Marcus, and Felix continue to be the best there but face oppression and discrimination because they are from the twelfth legion, the bottom of them all. One day they are accosted by a member of the Praetorians (the first legion) named Brennus, but Percy stops any violence. A while later the members of their cohort in the twelfth have begun to bond and become proficient at battle. They are sent to run around Agogia when a group of Praetorians led by Brennus attacks them, and are about to stab Castor when Macer shoves Castor out of the way and takes the knife. The praetorians flee and the twelfth are forced to watch Macer die.

When Tribune Gaius arrives he is furious and marches them to the first legion area, where he knocks out the guards who try to stop him and takes Brennus to punish (which is allowed, but due to class divides many see no issue with Brennus killing a member of the twelfth). He whips him badly before Imperator Tyrus arrives, leader of all the legions, and demands the boy back from Gaius, who reluctantly agrees. Gaius warns the legion there will be further punishment against them for trying to get even with their betters. The only bright side is that this death bonds the convicts like Anas with Castor and his friends.

At the end of the Fidelis training Castor, Marcus, and Felix are clearly the best soldiers and users of iron on the field, but the “iron council” which consists of a bunch of rich and famous palatines, decides to deny them and allow three praetorians, including Brennus, to take their place as silvers. Shortly after this their cohort of the twelfth legion gets sent to the far north despite not having the training, presumably as punishment for Gaius’ actions.

Percy comes with them and continues to train the three friends since he knows it was unfair they were denied silver. As they march in the snow Castor and his group are attacked by a Norman raiding party, and Castor is almost killed by a large ice golem, a Furia, but manages to attune the symbol of Ancient Frost and shatter the thing. This is not supposed to be possible, as even people with multiple hearts can not attune multiple symbols. Castor does his best to keep it a secret out of fear of the empire torturing him to figure out how this happened.

They reach Frigidus, the fortress guarding the empire’s northern border from the Normans, and are forced by the legions orders to keep scouting north and figure out why the Normans are being more aggressive. Percy leads a scouting party of Castor, Anas, and the collegium students from Segesta. They get attacked by a Norman Berserker, who is able to cut them off from their symbols, leaving them powerless. Percy manages to save them but several die in the fight, and Clintus breaks his leg. Percy is forced to leave them to warn the cohort and get reinforcements but their small group gets captured by a berserker woman, Astrid, while he is gone.

They get brought to a large Norman camp, and learn they have united under a High King, Knut. Knut gives them to a strange palatine woman, Vixa, with strange black Cor heart veins. She takes them to a cave and they learn she is a Necromanta, thought to be just a myth. Castor grabs his ancient frost symbol, and suddenly finds himself somewhere else. He has been brought before the supposedly dead god of cold, Aquilo. Aquilo makes him swear a Votum to kill the Necromanta and throw her body into a pit, then gives Castor the power of an ice blade to focus his energy and summon the gods wrath to kill the high king and his army. Castor is basically forced to agree and manages to kill Vixa using both his symbols.

The group armors up and continues sneaking around in caves, finding where the Normans create the Furia golems. They kill all the people and disguise themselves before trying to flee the camp. They eventually meet up with rest of their cohort, who have been trapped by the Norman forces.

Gaius challenges the High King to a duel, where the winner will be allowed to leave peacefully, and Knut agrees, despite the protestations of two palatine empire looking people who Gaius recognizes. Gaius and Knut have a close fight, and one of the palatines tries to control the snow to trip Gaius, but Gaius is still able to win and cuts off Knut’s head. Instead of being allowed to leave however the palatines make the Normans attack, so Castor summons Aquilo’s wrath, causing a massive winter storm.

Their cohort flees, losing many people to the Norman Berserkers, and are escaping down a mountain pass when they get ambushed. They are fighting when an avalanche sweeps them all away. Castor uses his ancient frost symbol to try to save those around him without knowing if they are friend or foe, and his leg breaks in the fall. When they manage to dig themselves out Castor finds Felix and Marcus alive, but then Astrid (the Norman Berseker) also digs out and some large Furia. Marcus begins dueling Astrid as the injured Felix and Castor hold of the Furia, but Astrid is able to stab and then kill Marcus. In his anger Castor shatters the inhibitus collar from his neck and ascends to silver in both his iron and frost symbol. With his new strength he kills the Furia. Felix is upset and angry and sad, believing that the empire will kill Castor for ascending without permission. They continue to argue but are cut off from their legion so begin walking, and in the storm they lose each other.

Castor feels his strength fading and passes out. He wakes up chained in a slavers cart. Because the chains are iron he could easily escape but has no idea what to do currently so stays with the slavers and plots his revenge on the empire. THE END.
