Red Rising
Pierce Brown
Red Rising #1

Red Rising Summary

Darrow is a Red, a member of the lowest caste, in a society set on expanding into the solar system. Darrow is a Helldiver, operating heavy machinery, for the Lambda clan in Lykos, a city under the surface of the planet Mars. The Reds are there to mine Helium-3 to allow others to terraform the surface of the planet and make it livable, and have been working towards this goal for generations. They are taught they are brave pioneers fighting to save their people since Earth is dying.

Within Lykos there are multiple clans, and Darrow finally mines enough to beat Gamma and earn his group the Laurel, which means they all receive extra food and supplies. Darrow and his young wife Eo go to the Laurel celebration, but Gamma is still given the Laurel, meaning none of the other groups ever have a chance to win it. Eo takes Darrow away to help reduce his anger, and takes him through a broken vent to a secret garden grown for the higher colors. They spend the night there and are happy, but when they return the Gray guards catch them. 

The Governor of Mars, Nero au Augustus, comes down to supervise their punishment, since Darrow is a helldiver. Darrow is whipped, but when Eo is brought up she instead sings the forbidden song and is sentenced to death. She is hanged, and Darrow is devastated so he breaks the law and takes her body from the noose, burying her. The punishment for this is also death, and Darrow’s Uncle Narol comes to take him to his own hanging, giving him something in a flask on the way. Darrow feels something is off but is hanged and sees his uncle pulling his feet (You have to pull people due to low gravity).

Darrow wakes up. He is confused but not dead, and a truck comes and takes him to what he assumes is the rebel group the Sons of Ares. A man named Dancer and a woman, Harmony, tell him they want his help and begin showing him the real world. They take him to the surface and show him Mars is already terraformed, and the other colors, even other Reds, live and work in a lush environment. The LowReds, the ones mining underground, have been lied to and oppressed for generations. The HighReds on the surface are menial servants working the worst jobs, all lorded over by the Golds who rule society. Darrow agrees to help Dancer and the Sons of Ares fight the society and hopefully stop the oppression.

They first take him to a Violet Carver named Mickey, and tell him to turn Darrow into a Gold. It is a long and incredibly painful process, but Darrow is transformed from the Red he grew up as into a Gold body. Next him and Harmony begin strength and endurance training, and Darrow begins working with Pink Matteo, who teaches him about manners and culture in the higher castes. They create a fake family and history for Darrow, but he insists on keeping his same first name. After months of prep work the now Gold and cultured Darrow is ready to enter the Institute, a mysterious school all the highest ranked Golds attend. Darrow excels in the entrance exams and meets a high-born boy named Cassius.

Darrow is accepted and on the way meets Julian and Sevro. All accepted students are lined up and there is a draft, with different “houses” named for Roman gods choosing the students they want. Darrow is selected 10th overall of the 1,000 students, by House Mars.

Later the 100 students sit down to a feast with their proctor, Fitchner, and Darrow learns Julian and Cassius are brothers. He also meets a cruel girl, Antonia. They go to sleep in their rooms and wake up being beaten by massive Obsidians. They are stripped and individually brought to small rooms. Darrow finds himself in a room with Julian, and someone throws in a ring and tells them only one can leave. Darrow is reluctant but kills Julian. He walks out to find a bunch of bloody, confused, and sad Gold teenagers who survived the fighting. They return to the feast table and learn the rest of their time in the institute will be in a large war simulation, with each house controlling a castle and competing to win. Cassius is livid Julian is dead and tries to figure out who did it but does not suspect Darrow.

They are dropped into the castle of House Mars, and begin trying to plan what to do, but are stuck with internal strife. Darrow and Cassius control part of the tribe, Antonia takes another chunk, and a massive, cruel boy named Titus takes the rest. The only one left out is Sevro, who is surviving very well on his own. Titus thrives on savagery and leads his people on raids of the nearby House Ceres castle but doesn’t find much success. Part of the game is that any captured people can be marked with your houses sigil using a house standard and have to act as slaves to your house or be considered Shamed and have their prospects after this reduced. As time passes Titus becomes more and more vicious, and starts abusing the slaves he has taken. He cuts off some of their ears and begins raping some of the girls. 

Darrow and Cassius have been mildly successful with their group, containing their friends Roque, Lea, and Quinn. At one point Titus manages to capture Quinn and cuts off her ear, sending Cassius into a rage but they capture and beat and piss on him before another boy, Pollux, helps him escape. Darrow and Cassius know something must be done, so they begin scouting and get caught by some members of House Minerva. Sevro comes and rescues them and they take the horses Minerva’s group had and raid their castle. They return with the Minerva standard to find that the House Minerva Primus, Virginia (Who Darrow calls Mustang), has taken their castle and taken Titus prisoner. They trade her their castle for her standard and she leaves, disgusted by what Titus has done. Darrow is basically leader at this point and knows something needs to be done.

He goes and and visits Titus in his cell and realizes by how he talks Titus is also a Red that was somehow raised to Gold, but he still must be punished. Cassius kills Titus, believing he killed Julian, and together Darrow and Cassius begin organizing and using house Mars to win. Darrow and Sevro go and negotiate with house Diana to help them take down Minerva. House Diana agrees and they make a plan.

Darrow and his army ride out from their castle to Minerva’s but will never be able to break their walls, so Darrow challenges their best fighter, the massive Pax, to a duel. Minerva checks for treachery but finds none and they agree to the duel. Darrow and Pax fight, and in the middle of it Darrow howls to signal his men and Sevro, a Diana boy Tactus, and others saw out of the dead horses they hid in and storm the open gates. Darrow beats Pax in the duel and they quickly take most of House Minerva prisoner, with only Mustang escaping. While searching Darrow finds her but lets her go fearing his army's vengeance. After the battle Lilath, a messenger from someone in the south called the Jackal, who leads House Pluto and much of the south, comes and tells Cassius he will be rewarded for bringing them Darrow dead or alive, and gives Cassius something.

Back at Castle Mars, Darrow should have been made Primus but for some reason the Proctors (who fly around supervising) have not done so. While celebrating, Lea runs in and tells Darrow they took Roque, and he rushes out with her to help, but realizes that this is a trap and hides in the mist. Antonia and her cronies look for him but don’t find him, so they kill Lea to try to bring him out. Darrow escapes and returns to his castle to tell them of the treachery. Him and Cassius ride out but Cassius tells him he knows Darrow killed Julian and challenges him to a duel. Cassius stabs Darrow in the stomach and leaves him to die.

Darrow wakes up to find Mustang caring for him and that she saved his life. They hide together through the winter. Darrow has realized that the proctors gave the Jackal a video of him killing Julian to break him and Cassius apart, and the game is rigged. He talks to Fitchner, the Mars Proctor, who tells him Jackal is the Governor’s son and he bribed the proctors to let him win. Darrow is disgusted and vows to be better. Him and Mustang begin gathering the runaway slaves and forming their own army. They sneak a bunch of slaves away from Mars, including Pax, and then take the Ceres Castle.

After the battle for Ceres, one of the freed slaves, Tactus, tries to rape a girl. Darrow punishes him by whipping him 20 times, but then also has Pax whip him 20 times to show anything his army does he is responsible for. This and his other actions earns him their undying loyalty, and they begin a march south to take out House Apollo (Darrow wants to enslave them all so their proctor has to leave the game). As they march the proctors begin interfering more and more, cutting their horses free and poisoning their food. Sevro saves Darrow from a trap set by Proctor Apollo and rejoins Darrow’s army. They quickly take Castle Apollo, but Fitchner comes to tell him stuff is going wrong and Proctor Apollo won’t leave the game. Darrow is still determined to win despite the cheating.

Darrow beats up Fitchner by bypassing his armor and takes some of his gear before trapping him in a cell, then him and some of his soldiers head to Castle Juno. They find a small contingent led by a boy named Lucian who surrenders. Darrow has his men pretend to be drunk and sends Pax out on some mission, talking to Lucian alone. Darrow quickly stabs Lucian's hand into the table and his men stop pretending to be drunk. Lucian is the Jackal, and Pax has captured and enslaved all the forces he had hidden ready to spring an ambush. The Jackal is cold and cruel, and Darrow tells him if he cuts off his own hand he can go, but the crazy boy actually does it. Right before the end Proctor Apollo throws a sonic bomb into the room, and in the chaos Pax jumps onto Darrow to protect him as the Jackal tries to stab him but can’t, and is forced to flee. 

Darrow, Sevro, and others are upset Pax is dead and chase after the Jackal, nearly getting him when the proctors cheat more. Apollo appears and pulls Darrow into the air, telling him how dumb he is. He drops Darrow but the gravBoots he took from Fitchner allow him to fly, and he stabs Apollo in the eye with Dancer’s knife ring. They take his gear and fly with his small contingent to Mount Olympus, the place the proctors live. They begin capturing the other proctors and getting more gear. Jupiter nearly kills Darrow but Sevro disables him and they free Mustang, who the proctors had taken captive. They begin arming their forces with all the high tech stuff the proctors had, knowing they need to win before any outside forces show up to stop them.

Darrow and Mustang kiss and he has her take half the army and loot and chase down the Jackal in a cave where he is hiding, while Darrow goes and conquers the last few students and House Mars. Darrow frees Fitchner who tells him Virginia is Lucian’s twin sister, daughter of Governor Nero. Darrow feels stupid and assumes she will betray him, so he quickly takes Mars back. Cassius spits on him and says they have a blood feud over Julian, and one of them will die next time. Darrow leads his new army and prepares for Mustang and Jackal to attack him, but Mustang returns with her brother bound and gagged. Darrow has won.

The governor and various important personages arrive. Nero pulls Darrow aside and they basically trade Darrow saying nothing about his cheating for more opportunity. Darrow, despite his hate for the man who hanged Eo, kneels and agrees to basically be his son and rise to whatever opportunity he can take. THE END.
