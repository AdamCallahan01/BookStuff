Too Like the Lightning
Ada Palmer
Terra Ignota #1

Too Like the Lightning Summary

Notes:
1. This will not be a great summary for understanding anything but the basic plot events, there is a lot of pieces I won’t be able to cover due to every scene being valuable
2. This is written by Mycroft Canner, so the book is a history written by him for future generations (After the year 2454)
3. Most people refer to others as “they” however Mycroft insists on using gendered pronouns for people, but does not always use the same gender the person biologically is, just what he feels fits, so that may be confusing to track

Mycroft starts by directly addressing the reader and saying, despite the events of the book happening in the year 2454 and the “reader” theoretically being further in the future, he will write as if in the 18th century French enlightenment. 

Sensayer Carlyle Foster gets assigned to the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash. Sensayer’s are general spirituality advisors, since specific religions are illegal in public, and a bash is basically an adopted family who live together, so you have ba’pas (bash parents), ba’sibs, etc. The Saneer-Weeksbooth bash is a Humanist bash (Humanist is one of 7 hives, the groups people can self elect into and have replaced nationality in many ways), and very important since it and its members control the “flying car system”, that allows people to travel across the globe in about an hour, making all of Earth very interconnected.

Carlyle accidentally walks into a scene in the bash after hearing screaming, and finds a tiny (like a couple inches tall) man dying. He is tackled by Mycroft (our narrator) and interrogated by Mycroft and Thisbe Saneer. Carlyle learns about the 13 year old boy Bridger, who has the power to make anything real. He can touch a picture and make the item reality. In this case he has made some toy soldiers real and one of them died when a cat attacked it. Thisbe and Mycroft swear Carlyle to secrecy, enforcing that Bridger is the most important thing on Earth.

Martin Guildbreaker arrives at the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash. He is a servant of the emperor Cornel Mason, (Masons are another hive), and was sent to investigate a break in. Someone stole the Black Sakura Seven-Ten list before it was published and left it in the Saneer-Weeksbooth Bash. Seven-Ten lists are basically a ranking of the 7 hives, naming the most important person in each hive, as well as 3 other people to make a total of 10 names. They are published individually by each hive, and the Black Sakura publishes the Mitsubishi hive list. 

Martin informs Ockham Saneer, the chief of security for the bash, that the culprit used what is commonly called the Canner Device (named after our narrator Mycroft Canner) to steal the identity of Ockham’s tracker (Everyone wears a little earclip thing that basically acts as a personal computer/phone/identifier), so it looked like Ockham broke into Black Sakura and stole the list but in reality it was some mystery person. Ockham is fairly combative about this since he really cares about the security of their car service, but lets Martin investigate since it is important.

Martin questions Thisbe and Mycroft, whom we learn is a “servicer”, someone who has committed crimes and is now forced to serve the world as penance. We don’t learn Mycroft Canner’s crimes for a while but it is repeatedly implied they were bad and fairly famous years ago. Mycroft is summoned to Togenkyo and takes off in a car.

Mycroft meets with Chief Director Hotaka Ando Mitsubishi, leader of the Mitsubishi hive. Him and his wife Danae question Mycroft about the break-in, especially since it used the device named for him years ago. Mycroft reveals to them he never actually had this tracker-swapping device, just the packaging, he actually had some other way to hide himself and his crimes. Because they forced that out of him he tells Danae that Carlyle, who is a gag-gene (Someone who’s DNA records are hidden), is her biological son, basically trading blackmail with them. An important note for later is that Ando and Danae have an adopted bash of children who are just now reaching adulthood and getting prominent positions in many places around the world in all the hives. One of their children actually wrote the seven-ten list that got stolen.

Carlyle, who is a Cousin (Hive traditionally made up of women that are basically caretakers for people around the world, nurses, teachers, sensayers), comes back to the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash due to their curiosity about Bridger, but ends up talking to Eureka Weeksbooth. Eureka is a set-set, specifically a Cartesian set-set, a child who from birth was raised in such a way (in this case basically attached to a computer it seems) to give them super-human skills of some manner. Eureka is always plugged in and monitoring the millions of super fast flying cars transporting people all over the world, while maintaining a text conversation with Carlyle. There is a lot of prejudice against the creation of set-sets, saying they aren’t really human anymore. Cato Weeksbooth, a mad scientist member of the bash, is terrified of Sensayers and runs away from Carlyle. Mycroft discusses more about Bridger and their powers with Carlyle, trying to calm them down and rationalize it. Mycroft gets called away again to another job.

Mycroft meets with the Censor Vivien Ancelet, and they run over the economic and political impact the Black Sakura theft and corresponding seven ten lists will have on the world. Many of the numbers spell doom for the Mitsubishi hive, and Mycroft and Vivien work hard to try and figure out how to prevent any potential conflict. The world has not seen war since the Church Wars (I think about 200-250 years ago) that ended with religion being outlawed. Some of the numbers they come up with, we learn Mycroft kind of predicted years ago when committing his crimes.

Dominic Seneschal, a compatriot of Martin Guildbreaker, and a sensayer, blacklaw (someone who asks for no protection from laws anywhere), and apparently investigator, shows up to check out the saneer-weeksbooth bash, then ends up disappearing. They seem to have a hint that someone (Bridger) has been here. We have heard references of the emperor Mason’s child, J.E.D.D. Mason, who Dominic and Martin serve.

Mycroft and Censor Vivien leave their machinations to attend the Renunciation Day ceremony, which celebrates when humans first decided to leave behind the nationality defined by borders and place of birth, and instead let people choose their own nationality, which eventually became hives. Vivien is married to Bryar Kosala, leader of the Cousins. They all head off to attend Duke Ganymede’s party in France this evening. Ganymede is president of the Humanist hive, but also tries to lay claim to noble french bloodlines, and is the brother to Danae.

At the party at La Tremoille, many of the influential and powerful show up and interact. There is a disturbance and Cato Weeksbooth appears dressed as Dr. Frankenstein and brings to life his monster, who turns out to Sniper, the most famous member of the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash. Sniper is famous for dressing as a doll and creating fake dolls of themselves, and wields a lot of soft power. Ganymede and Sniper briefly address the Black Sakura theft and both say they are innocent. The leader of the Gordian hive, and headmaster of the Brillist institute, Felix Faust arrives, and him, Ganymede, Cornel Mason, Bryar Kosala, and Ando Mitsubishi discuss the break in and how the seven ten lists will affect global politics.

While working at the party Mycroft overhears some violent words and goes and scares off some drunks who were harassing two utopians, the last hive. The utopians, Aldrin Bester and Voltaire Seldon, tell Mycroft they plan to help investigate the Black Sakura theft and will track down whoever is using the tracker-shifting device. 

A quick run down of the 7 hives:
Masons - Led by the emperor Cornel Mason, largest hive with 3 billion and change
Utopians - A small, mysterious hive focused on technology and advancement, currently trying to colonize mar, led by The Anonymous
Humanists - Led by President Ganymede, they focus on human excellence and creativity
European Union - led by elected Casimir Perry, and the King of Spain (Carlos II) also has influence
Mitsubishi - Led by Director Ando, they own 2/3s of land on Earth and some people are resentful of that
Cousins - Led by Bryar Kosala, formed from a group of women helping others, they rule by citizen suggestions
Gordian/Brillist - Led by Felix Faust, don’t wield much political power.

There is a security “drill” at the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash, where Ockham finds something is off with a group of humanist soldiers. It is also discovered Dominic Seneschal is still in the bash somewhere, and has been overriding the security to keep themselves hidden. J.E.D.D. Mason shows up, a very odd figure who seems somewhat disconnected from their body. Jed interrogates the seemingly traitorous soldiers and discovers they meant no harm, but merely were trying to locate the Canner Device, which was reportedly activated for a minute or two in the bash earlier. Jed seems to be able to look into people’s souls, or read their minds, or something along those lines. They leave the investigation to Ockham and depart.

Carlyle again comes to meet with Bridger, Mycroft, and Thisbe. They have a lot of discussions about Bridger’s importance, if Bridger should be allowed to bring back their dead toy soldier (Since Bridger can just imagine into existence a revive potion or something), and JEDD Mason. Bridger ends up bringing back the soldier, who says they don’t remember being dead but are grateful to return. Mycroft reveals the person he serves above all others is JEDD, which causes suspicion and surprise from the other two adults. Carlyle is worried due to comments Jed made during the security investigation that they may be practicing religion or encouraging it in others so he and Thisbe begin looking into them. Mycroft gets called away, but Bridger does not want them to leave. Dominic found Bridger’s hidden cave and stole and broke a lot of stuff. Mycroft makes plans for what Bridger needs to do if he ever needs to flee.

Mycroft is summoned to a meeting of the leaders of the Mitsubishi Hive with Jed Mason. Mycroft explains that back in his criminal days he only ever bought the packaging for the so-called “Canner Device” in order to make people think that was how he was evading detection. He says he bought it from some Japanese people, and after bickering the leaders decide it was most likely built to frame the Japanese. Director Ando fears one of his predecessors may have had it built. Jed is able to tell if people are lying and confirms Ando’s innocence. They also try to figure out how to best handle the fall-out from the Black Sakura theft once the seven-ten lists are released.

Carlyle and Thisbe use the car records Thisbe has access to, and with help from Eureka to analyze them, are able to track down a few places where Jed, Martin Guildbreaker, and Dominic all frequent. They go to the first one and meet Gibraltar Chagatai, who lives in what seems to be a converted old church. Gibraltar explains how Jed, Dominc, and Martin are basically a pseudo-bash together. Carlyle sneaks around the place and finds a bunch of scavenged religious items, making him far more concerned about Jed leading a cult.

Mycroft gets worried when Bridger won’t respond to his messages and rushes back to the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash. He finds Bridger distraught, since Dominic came super close to catching him this time. He is trying to comfort Bridger when Thisbe and Carlyle show up and confront him about Jed maybe practicing religion illegally. While Mycroft tries to calm Bridger and reassure them about JEDD Mason, Ockham comes to the door and talks to Thisbe through it, telling her whatever she is doing is risking attracting attention to the bash when they do not need it. He knows Mycroft is there so says (again he is not in the room) that if the media learns Mycroft Canner is here it will be a big deal. This is the first time Carlyle has heard Mycroft’s last name, and immediately looks to Mycroft’s right ear to see a hole, identifying him for who he is.

Carlyle then freaks out and goes a bit crazy about this being THE Mycroft Canner. Bridger tries to defend Mycroft, saying he has changed, but they have to hide Bridger and Ockham comes in and tells Carlyle he can not tell anyone. Carlyle begins spouting Mycroft’s crimes and we learn for the first time the beginning of what Mycroft has done. As a child his bash house all died, except him, in a fire, and he went to live with a foster bash, the Terrafirma cousin bash. That foster bash was close friends with the Mardi bash, a famous and powerful group. When they neared adulthood Mycroft kidnapped, tortured, shared what he did, and then killed 17 members of the Mardi bash. Carlyle says how he ate the heart of one while they still lived. We learn more bits and pieces over time but Mycroft committed some of the most awful torture and crimes imaginable, he is a terrible person. Mycroft gets summoned by the Emperor so must leave, but Ockham forces Carlyle to stay since any who know Mycroft was not executed are ordered to silence.

After Ockham leaves Bridger and Carlyle argue for a while about Mycroft, Bridger defending him saying he has changed and has cared for Bridger almost his whole life. Carlyle can’t get over the horror of his crimes. Thisbe and Carlyle plan to leave but then Julia Doria-Pamphili arrives, the head of the sensayer conclave and Carlyle’s teacher. She also happens to be Mycroft’s personal sensayer so also helps talk down Carlyle, and offers to let him meet with Bryar Kosala, head of the Cousins. Julia and Carlyle leave, and in the car we learn they are more politically active than suspected, especially Carlyle.

Mycroft meets with the Emperor Cornel Mason, Jed Mason, and the two utopians from earlier, Voltaire and Aldrin. They also discuss impacts of the seven-ten list, who did the crime, the canner device, and how best to protect the car system that is run from the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash. The Utopians have their own car system that is slower but slightly safer, and the emperor asks them to be ready to take over if needed.

Later, Mycroft is walking alone when he gets dragged into an alley. We meet Saladin, his lover and someone no one else knows is alive. Saladin warns Mycroft that Tully Mardi, the last living member of the bash who has been hiding in Luna City on the moon, is coming back. Saladin wants to kill Tully, but Mycroft says he can not although he wishes Saladin good luck. He also briefs Saladin on Bridger and asks him to be ready to help. Saladin flees and the cops show up due to Mycroft’s tracker detecting his elevated heart-rate. Police Commissioner General Ektor Carlyle Papadelias (Papa) talks to Mycroft, who claims something called the Canner Beat knocked him out. We later learn that the Canner Beat is made from some old recordings Mycroft made and when he hears it his mind goes into the past and his body shuts down. Papa is the one who hunted and caught Mycroft, but still feels he is missing something. The two are sort of friends now and Mycroft always has answers and alibis to his questions, but all this time has been hiding the existence of Saladin, his co-conspirator in the murders, which is what Papa can’t quite piece together.

Carlyle and Thisbe go to the second place associated with Jed Mason, what Eureka refers to as the Black Hole, since the car records for people going and leaving are odd. Mycroft gets a notification when they near the place and is horrified, but hacks into Carlyle’s tracker to supervise and begins rushing over. Carlyle and Thisbe are immediately shocked, since people inside are dressed not in the gender-neutral outfits of the 25th century but as if they are in the 18th century, and address each other, as well as Thisbe and Carlyle, by gendered pronouns. They meet the nun Heloise, which has Carlyle very distraught since she clearly practices religion in the open. They are also flabbergasted to learn that J.E.D.D. Mason’s first name is Jehovah, an openly religious name. Mycroft arrives and they are brought to the madam of the house, Mycroft rapidly trying to warn and prepare them on the way, even telling Thisbe to bash his head and flee but she does not.

They meet Madame D’Arouet, and learn that this place is a spot for people to indulge in non-appropriate ideas in the modern world. It is partly a brothel, but also a place to discuss religion and forbidden ideas. Carlyle is even more horrified but is reassured that sensayers and Bryar Kosala know about it and come to supervise it. It is better to have these ideas in one central place than spoken in secret around the world. We learn that JEDD’s full name is Jehovah Epicurus Donatien D’Arouet, and he was named by multiple world leaders, since he represents a bond tying many hives together. The Madame wields significant soft power, and most of the world leaders come here often to discuss politics together. Thisbe and Carlyle are mostly appeased and are allowed to leave.

Mycroft is working with some fellow servicers when they stumble into a place Tully Mardi is speaking by chance. He recognizes Mycroft immediately and sends a mob after him. Mycroft has made himself a leader among the servicers and they try to protect him, but someone plays the canner beat and he is rendered immobile. A group of utopians appear, who had been invisibly watching and protect Mycroft, taking him away in a car. (Utopians wear coats of Griffincloth, which normally show various images of the surroundings but can also make them invisible).

Mycroft is taken to Julia Doria-Pamphili’s office for a sensaying session, and she orders him to rest in her closet for a few hours. At some point Dominic Seneschal arrives (Not knowing Mycroft is here), and tells Julia he has found god. They begin having sex and discussing religion and politics, with Dominic asking for permission to “have” Carlyle, and saying if this is allowed he will show Julia the god he has found soon. Julia reveals there is a witness in the closet and Dominic becomes enraged, nearly killing her and planning to kill the witness but when finding it Mycroft is happy. Dominic takes Mycroft and interrogates him about Bridger (without knowing how Bridger is), but Mycroft keeps deflecting. We learn Dominic kidnapped a toy soldier and is trying to torture information out of it.

Saladin finds Bridger outside his cave, distraught over something Dominic has done. Saladin is wearing an invisible griffincloth cloak and convinces Bridger to trust him and then goes to look at the cave. Dominic has perfectly recreated one of the murders Mycroft (And secretly Saladin) carried out in the past on one of the toy soldiers. Dominic left a note for Bridger telling him to meet them at a specific location, which Saladin plans to do and kill Dominic. The Major (one of Bridger’s toy soldiers), warns Saladin Dominic is incredibly dangerous.

Most of the world leaders hold a meeting at Madame D’Arouet’s brothel. Mycroft and Jedd mason are there, along with the King of Spain and the Emperor, both of whom are in love with the Madame, Bryar Kosala and The Anonymous (who are each having affairs with each other), Danae and Director Ando, as well as Duke Ganymede, the three of whom seem to have an incestuous three-way relationship thing going on. Felix Faust is also there, and publicly calls out Danae and Ando for sending their adopted children into power positions everywhere. Apparently the foster kids were all trained to be set-sets but kicked out early due to a Nurturist agenda shutting down their program. They each then claim innocence for the Black Sakura theft and Jed says everyone is being honest. The anonymous and Faust both let people think their lists were also tampered with to help lower the impact of the theft and subsequent list reveals. After a lot of discussion they agree to bring European leader Casimir Perry into their little circle to help plan the future of the world. We learn Mycroft also killed someone called Apollo Mojave, who most of the world leaders loved, making it even stranger Cornel Mason let him live.

Carlyle returns to the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash to find a group of servicers being harassed by a small mob. News about Mycroft Canner being one of them has gotten out and people are taking that anger out on all servicers. Carlyle defends them then goes down to talk to them. They are busy moving all of Bridger's stuff to a location in Sniper’s doll museum. Carlyle is shown a second cave of Bridger’s, where he has been storing tons of lost toys he recovered from the trash, and presumably some stuff he drew and brought to life. Carlyle has an epiphany and realizes that JEDD Mason must also be like Bridger, or else Mycroft would not worship him so. The lead servicer warns Carlyle Dominic is after him, planning to kidnap and rape Carlyle, but Carlyle is set on meeting with JEDD Mason anyway.

End of the main story.

We also got several interludes from Martin Guildbreaker, but the last one at the end of the book is most important. Martin earlier interviewed Cato Weeksbooth and learned Cato is mentally unstable and has tried to kill themselves multiple times, and that there have been some strange suicides associated with Cato and the Black Sakura theft. Cato’s old sensayer, Emerald Revere, killed themselves opening the door for Carlyle to come to the bash. Martin meets with Police chief Papadelias and they go over a bunch of files. The data seems to show that of the like 70ish car crashes in the last 5 years, half of them have been politically significant. Combined with some other data they both believe that the Saneer-Weeksbooth bash, either on their own orders or from someone else, has been killing specific people to stop political unrest, potentially saving thousands of lives. Also, the reason Ockham, Thisbe, and these other 20-30 year olds are in charge is all their elder bash members died in a rafting accident 5 years ago, which likely was not an accident. They don’t have enough evidence to prove it but clearly something is going on with how the world is staying stable. THE END.
