How to be a Farmer in a Fantasy World
Shawn Wyatt

Some guy named Drake something leaves Calderon City because he caught some noble embezlling money, and moves out to Arcadia Village, where he is given a free farm but has to make it profitable within a single year. They want people to move to the village because a dungeon just appeared by it so adventurers are showing up. He plants his first crops then explores the basement and fins a magic book that gives him the farmer class. He also has a magic river pool on his land that heals him and mana flowers that make everything grow faster and better. He has to go into to to repair his tools and meet Landry the sheriff, Arvil the blacksmith, Finley the merchant, and Hazel a local taverngirl who likes him. HE continues to plant crops and get better at farming, but oh no there is a 1,000 coin bounty for him posted by the noble from the start. He meets an adventurer named Melody and they start dating. He is about to make the farm profitable when the bounty gets raised to 50,000 coins and another adventurer who likes Hazel comes to collec t it. Melody stops him but he burns down Drakes crops, so he will not meet the profit deadline. Luckily magic sprites deus ex machina everything adn the bounty goes away and happy ever after.
