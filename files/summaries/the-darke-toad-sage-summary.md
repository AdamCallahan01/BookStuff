The Darke Toad
Angie Sage
Septimus Heap #1.5

The Darke Toad Summary

Simon Heap has apprenticed himself to DomDaniel, and is currently away in the Badlands trying to perform a Darke ritual to give his master, who is currently just bones, some sort of body back. He somewhat succeeds on his bones clothes spell and DomDaniel is able to move around on his own.  They head to the Port to try and get some more help.

Marcia decided to bring her new apprentice Septimus to the Port to learn more about the world outside the castle, but they are surprised to find many people dressed up in costumes, and most people do not think she is actually the ExtraOrdinary wizard. 

Simon and DomDaniel arrive at a run-down house and meet the Port Coven witches. DomDaniel collapses back into a pile of bones and tells the Witch Mother, Pamela, that he will give her a Darke toad in exchange for a permanent clothes bones spell. They agree, although Pamela knows she can not do this permanently. DomDaniel has a substantial body again.

The witches decide they want a servant, so plan to get a Rat, a young ship’s boy, to come work for them. In order to get one on land, one of the witches, Linda, takes another one of the witches, Daphne’s, worms to the dock. They get the people celebrating Halloween involved, who think this is just a performance, and transform the worms into gribbles, termite-esque worms that eat ships. All the ships in the harbor sink and while everyone, Marcia included, is trying to save people, the witches kidnap a young boy, Jakey Fry.

Septimus spots them and gets Marcia and they chase after them, following DomDaniel’s toad to stay on the trail. Simon stepped away to get a horse for his master so they do not see him, but they encounter the bickering witches. Marcia manages to set Jakey free and sets a spell on the witches so they speak their minds for 24 hours. Septimus and Marcia leave, and DomDaniel gives the witches the Darke Toad, right before their spell fails and he collapses back into a pile of bones. Simon wraps him in his cloak and they return to the Badlands. THE END.
