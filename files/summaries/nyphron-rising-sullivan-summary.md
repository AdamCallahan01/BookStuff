Nyphron Rising
Michael J. Sullivan
The Riyria Revelations #3

Nyphron Rising Summary

Amilia is a scullery maid in the castle in Aquesta, the capital of the New Empire. She is working in the kitchens when a noble woman rushes down and tells them to ready a table for the empress. An unhealthily skinny, quiet girl wanders in, and when she won’t eat the noble lady slaps her, just as Regent (bishop) Saldur walks in. He orders the woman taken away and randomly appoints Amilia the new secretary to the empress in his frustration. Thrace, the girl who slew the beast in the previous book, has been renamed Modina Novronian and is clearly dealing with PTSD or something, since she never speaks and seems sick. Amilia goes with Modina to her room (which is more like a cell) and just talks to the girl about her life most of the night, gradually bringing in more and more comfort and asking Ibis the cook to make her easy broth soup to eat.

The Imperial army of the New Empire is camped outside of the capital of Melengar, and Hadrian and Royce are working for King Alric. They intercept some letters saying the Imperial army is ordered to retreat due to Degan Gaunt and the Nationalists invading Rhenydd. Hadrian has been melancholic lately and feels like his life does not have the purpose he used to dream of having. 

Princess Arista arrives back after many months of failed diplomatic missions trying to find allies for Melengar to fight against the empire. Upon returning home Alric is upset with her lack of progress and they get into a fight, with Alric saying he will send someone else as his next ambassador. Arista is furious and seeks out Royce, hiring him and Hadrian to escort her to Degan Gaunt ahead of her brother’s ambassador so she can negotiate a treaty between the Nationalist army and Melengar, hoping to trap the Imperials between them. Hadrian is still wanting more in life and tells Royce this is his last mission, he is retiring from Riyria after this. They begin travelling anonymously and Arista struggles mightily with the accommodations but quickly adapts to a non-castle lifestyle.

Back in Aquesta, Amilia is still trying her best to make life better for Modina, but not seeing a ton of progress besides her eating more. One day they go to the kitchens and Modina is playing with a dog when Regent Saldur walks in, and upon seeing the empress “demeaning” herself he orders Amilia taken away, but Modina speaks for the first time and says no, so Amilia is given more responsibility. Saldur meets with someone named Merrick Marius. He was hired by Luis Guy, leader of the Seret Knights who hunt the true heir of Novron. Merrick promises to stop the allying of Melengar and the nationalists and help save the empire.

Arista, Royce, and Hadrian arrive at Sheridan University, where Arista went to college, and meet with Arcadius, her favorite teacher. It turns out Royce and Hadrian also know him, and he actually helped introduce them to each other. He tells them about the Theorem Eldership, the group who still secretly try to protect the heir of Novron from the Seret Knights and the church. The real Royce brought them here is to ask about the image the wizard Esrahaddon showed him of Hadrian being the current generation of the Heir’s bodyguard. Arcadius confirmed he thought it was likely true.

They continue to travel, with Arista becoming more used to hardship, and reach Colnora, the base of Royce’s ex-gang, the Black Diamond. They ask for help arranging a meeting with Degan Gaunt and the gang agrees to help since it will hopefully mean the Imperial’s leave their city. One gang member, Etcher, is appointed to help them escape and they take off down the river, but are trapped by a couple guards. Arista uses her limited magical abilities to make them sneeze and Hadrian is able to kill them and they continue away. They plan to meet up with Etcher in Hintindar (Hadrian’s home village) after he arranges the meeting with Gaunt. 

They reach Hintindar, a tiny village, and Hadrian is greeted warmly (aside from one punch) by his old friends, who were upset he ran away from town. They stay with Hadrian’s old best friend, the town baker Dunstan. Hadrian upset the Imperial Liaison staying in the manor house, and guards are sent for all three of them. Etcher shows up right then and Royce sends Arista with him while he goes to find Hadrian. Royce decides just to get caught to find him and ends up getting thrown in a pit with Hadrian. While down there Royce tells Hadrian about how him and Esrahaddon believe he is the heir of Jeresh, part of the line of Teshlor knights meant to guard the heir of Novron. Royce was afraid of Hadrian leaving since he is one of the few people he cares about. They also realize Etcher must be a traitor and working with the Imperials, and quickly escape the prison pit and chase after him.

Meanwhile, Etcher had brought Arista to some ruins and handed her over to some men working for Luis Guy and the Seret Knights. After Etcher leaves Arista is terrified what the men will do to her and tries to work some more of her limited magic, which seems to be growing in strength. Royce and Hadrian arrive shortly after to find the men dead, Arista boiled their blood inside them. She is distraught over killing two men but together they head to Ratibor (Royce’s hometown).

Amilia has been ordered by Regent Saldur to make Modina give a speech in 3 days, since she has never really appeared before the public before and some people suspect that she is either not real or a puppet for Saldur and the church (Which is the truth). Amilia is given a temporary noble rank, and has a traveling courtier work as the empress's tutor. Together they move her up to a room on the fifth floor of the castle and having a window is Modina’s favorite thing (since she is never allowed to leave the castle). They continue to make life better for her and Amilia helps her memorize the short speech.

Ratibor is a poor, dirty place packed with refugees from the Nationalist Army’s invasion. The first night they can’t even find a room but are in a tavern where they hear a young man, Emery, trying to turn the crown against the occupying Imperials and support the Nationalists. Guards burst in and begin arresting people and Arista, Royce, and Hadrian are led into the sewers by someone working for the Black Diamond. They are taken to a safe house but learn Degan Gaunt has disappeared, and they assume he was led into a trap just like Arista when they thought they were meeting each other. Royce figures out this must be Merrick masterminding the plan (They used to be best friends but now are enemies). Arista comes up with a plan, they will do what Emery wanted and turn the city against the Imperials, while Hadrian leads the Nationalist army against the Imperial force outside the city, and Royce takes a message to King Alric to tell him to attack from the north.

Before starting all of that though, they follow clues left in a note from Hadrian’s father and find a church led by Monsignor Bartholomew. When he learns who Hadrian’s father was he tells them he is a member of the Theorem Eldership, and years ago he received word the heir was coming here. He helped them get a room, and the Heir, his bodyguard (Hadrian’s dad), and the heir’s pregnant wife all went there, but then Seret Knights showed up and started going into each building. The heir’s wife was giving birth so they could not flee, and Hadrian’s father fought to defend the heir. He was doing well but the knights snuck into the building and killed the heir’s wife and his newborn child, sending him into a rage and he died while fighting the knights. Hadrian’s father managed to kill them all and live, but feeling he failed his mention he went into hiding in Hintindar, where he had Hadrian and taught him the sword. Hadrian is sad again because he thinks the heir is gone, however Royce does not think so due to what Esrahaddon showed him.

Amilia is super nervous the day of Modina’s speech, but despite things going wrong, Modina (Who seems more like Thrace), delivers the speech prepared for her very well, but also personally thanks her friend, Lady Amilia, basically giving Amilia immunity from Saldur for the time being. Amilia and Modina return to her room.

Royce frees Emery and they begin to gather trustworthy leaders in Ratibor to organize the resistance. Arista uses her growing magic to make it rain constantly so the Imperial cavalry won’t be as effective. They plan to storm the armory and make a stand there. Hadrian heads out to the Nationalist army, but upon meeting with the current leader, a quarter master forced into the role when Gaunt vanished, does not trust him and ties him up. Hadrian thinks all is lost but Esrahaddon shows up and frees him. Apparently he has been helping the Nationalist army this whole time, and he makes them appoint Hadrian commander. They attack the Imperialist camp, and Esrahaddon uses magic to make them sleep so the battle is a rout.

Inside the city, they take the armory but people are nervous when the sheriff and garrison troops show up. Emery is uniting them but gets shot with a crossbow and dies. People are about to give up but Arista grabs Emery’s word and unites them, and they battle. Hadrian rides in shortly after to save the day and Ratibor is freed from the Imperials. Arista is devastated since she was beginning to love Emery. In the aftermath Hadrian talks to a woman named Miranda Gaunt, who tells him her adopted brother Degan Gaunt has the same necklace as Hadrian. She explains how her mother was a midwife at the same fateful birth where Hadrian’s father fought the Seret Knights and the heir died, however his wife had twins, unknown to all, and the midwife saved the first child and brought him to live with her own family. Hadrian feels like he has a new purpose and sets off the next morning, believing Degan Gaunt to be the heir of Novron.

Modina sees out her window a prisoner be brought to the castle, and Saldur and Merrick talk about their success and that they plan to keep him alive in the dungeons for some reason (presumably Degan Gaunt). On a road in the middle of nowhere, Arcadius and Miranda meet in secret. They discuss Merrick being an unexpected variable, and Miranda says Hadrian believed her every word. Arcadius says that is good, and that Royce and Hadrian must never learn the truth (whatever that means, very mysterious). THE END.
