I'm Not The Hero
Tommy Kerper
I'm Not The Hero #1

Orrin and his popular, athletic best friend Daniel are walking home when a truck hits them and they get sent to a fantasy video game world, except Orrin gets error messages and goes through a class creation screen. Daniel is just assigned “Hero”. They are both level 1 in the woods. They have quests. They head to the city of Day, and on the way meet Lady Madeleine and her guard. Her noble family helps them out and stuff. Orrin has a broken mana glitch from his class, Hero’es are really rare etc. They level stupidly fast, kill lots of stuff, make some  friends, expose some corruption.
