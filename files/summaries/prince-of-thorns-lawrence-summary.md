Prince of Thorns
Mark Lawrence
Broken Empire #1

Prince of Thorns Summary

Brother Jorg is travelling with his group of bandits, the brothers, and they just slaughtered the farming village of Mabberton. Jorg is only 13 years old, but already commands respect among a savage group of criminals. Jorg tells them they will take the Lich Road to their next destination, and when one of his “brothers” annoys him, he kills the man.

As they travel Jorg is reading Greek philosophy, implying this is set on Earth. As they travel they discover an old priest in a gibbet, and Jorg recognizes him as Father Gomst, the castle priest. They free him and he identifies Jorg as Prince Honorous Jorg Ancrath (I believe Honorous is part of his title as prince, but I am not 100% sure), and Brother Makin is actually Guard Captain Makin Bortha. They take the priest with them as they travel.

The dead rise on the Lich Road, and as the ghosts scare the other men, Jorg faces one down and scares it, causing the dead to flee. At this point we begin getting flashback chapters but I will condense them all here. Jorg was travelling with his mother the queen and his brother prince William when they were attacked by Count Renar’s men. Jorg was thrown into a patch of hook briar and trapped, forced to watch as the men killed his mother and brother. He was rescued later by his fathers men and brought to heal, but almost died many times. He had several fits and killed an assistant, Inch, in a frenzy. His tutor, Lundist, tried to steer him onto a better path but he was consumed by revenge. After learning that his father accepted some land rights instead of pursuing vengeance for his murdered wife he decides he has had enough. He asks Lundist to show him the dungeons of the Tall Castle where a group of men are being kept.

They go to the dungeons and find a Nuban man being tortured. Jorg frees one of his hands and tells him to show him how dangerous he is. The Nuban frees himself and his other brothers and Jorg leaves with them, quickly establishing his dominance and role in the group. The Nuban says he owes Jorg a life and Jorg wants his help killing Count Renar in revenge. Shortly after escaping Jorg and the Nuban are trapped in their sleep by a “dream-witch”, but Jorg breaks out and distracts the assassin sent for them while the Nuban kills him, taking the man's oversized crossbow as his own.

Back in the present, Jorg and his brothers see a group of Count Renar’s men approaching and Jorg tells them to prepare traps and such. He rides out under a flag of truce, finds Count Renar’s son Marclo and kills him, stealing his horse and riding back to his men. Using their traps and savagery they kill many of the enemy, and drink beer to celebrate the revealed Prince Jorg’s 14th birthday. Jorg decides it is time he returns home.

Jorg returns home to Tall Castle, and is brought before a pagan warlock Sageous, who seems to have some strange control over his father King Olidan. Jorg almost kills the man but flees due to some spell, which he only breaks later in the night. The next morning he meets a pretty girl named Katherine, who he learns is technically his aunt by marriage, since his father married her older sister. They are brought before the king and court, and the king mostly ignores Jorg, berating Makin for failing to bring the prince home. His new wife is pregnant and he seems to want that child to be heir. The king forces Makin to fight his new champion, Sir Galen, in a duel, and despite being a great swordsman Makin is losing, so Jorg pushes over a huge crystal tree that Sageous made, stopping the fight. He stands up to his father and tells him he will fight Galen. Galen tries to kill him but Jorg runs, grabs a guard's crossbow, and shoots Galen in the head, killing him.

His father meets with Jorg in private and tells him he wants Jorg to bring him Castle Red, the seat of Gelleth, and he only gives him the 200 men of the forest watch, a near impossible task. Jorg grabs a book on the area and he, Makin, and the brothers go to a tavern while Jorg plans their next move. Jorg learns people around that area seem to have deformities, such as being red, and the book makes him think there must be old biological warfare weapons somewhere. They go to the forest watch and Jorg kills Lord Vincent, the old captain, and Captain Coddin, their new leader.

He tells Codding to take the watch to the forest around Gelleth and the Castle Red, spreading rumors that Jorg is with them and causing havoc. Meanwhile, he will take his brothers to the gorge of the Leucrota. The notable brothers are Makin, The Nuban (who never gets a name), and Rike, a massive, evil man. The Leucrota come to meet them, deformed people who look more like monsters, led by the massive Gorgoth and a glowing-girl Jane. Jorg gives them Father Gomst in exchange for Gorgoth leading them to the Great Stair and the necromancers (A back way into the Castle Red).

They bring two Leucrota children who Gorgoth plans to sacrifice to the necromancers, but Jorg names them Gog and Megog and they have a fighting spirit. They reach the top and find a necromancer, but instead of working with them Jorg attacks and his men begin killing the dead. Several die along with the young boy Megog. Jorg chases the necromancer and finds she has the Nuban hostage over a pit. Jorg actually seems to care about the Nuban but still shoots the massive crossbow he left through the Nuban and into the necromancer, sending them both falling into the pit. He returns to his men to find the fight over, but brawls with Rike over a comment about the Nuban, establishing himself as their leader. He invites Gog to join their group and Gorgoth continues leading them.

They find one more necromancer but Jorg kills him before anything can happen, and eats his heart. They enter the caves under the Castle Red, and Jorg follows a map from his book and leads them to a huge steel door, made by “The Builders”. All this seems to mean this is set hundreds or thousands of years in the future after some sort of nuclear apocalypse. They activate an ancient computer locking the door and talk to it before finding the door unlocked. Inside are crates of some sort of weapon. Jorg initially wants to light it all and destroy the world but they talk him down into just doing a single crate. 

They are leaving back the way they came when suddenly the mountain behind them explodes. They get caught in the dust cloud from the blast, and while unconscious Jorg hears a conversation between Jane, Sageous, and the necromancer he thought fell into the pit. He realizes there is a bigger game being played here, and him eating the necromancer heart seems to have given him some sort of power. 

They return to the Tall Castle and Jorg shows them the dust of Castle Red, then when he is distracted his father stabs him in the heart, and Jorg slowly passes out. 

Flashback: Jorg and the Nuban went to Count Renar’s castle, and tried to meet with his magician/bodyguard Corion to learn how to get by his defenses. Corion did something to both of them, putting a spell on the Nuban’s crossbow, and told Jorg to focus his anger instead on the rest of the world. He had forgotten about this whole venture until just now, when he seems on the verge of death, and realizes he has not been pursuing Count Renar like he initially wanted to, and seems to break free of the spell. 

Jorg begins to come to his senses and hears Katherine talking with her maid Hanna. Hanna wants to just smother him since they can tell he is still alive but Katherine won’t let her. Later Jorg awakens with hands around his throat and fights back, strangling his assailant. He fully wakes up and finds Hanna dead beneath him. Katherine shows up and accuses him of murder, and he is forced to knock her out and take her dagger. He goes down to the dungeons and frees Makin, who seems very weak and apparently several weeks have passed. Together they escape and head to Count Renar’s castle. He is holding a tournament, so Jorg kills Sir Alain Kennick, taking his horse and armor and posing as him to enter the tourney.

He finds the remaining brothers and some of the forest watch and asks them to remain loyal to him, and as king he will remain loyal in return. They agree to help him by distracting men and holding certain points the following day. Jorg, disguised still as Sir Alain, enters the tourney and goes on a killing spree, causing chaos, and then his men begin attacking the Count’s people in the crowd. He is going for Count Renar when Corion uses a spell to freeze him in place. He watches as his men begin to die, and Corion is about to kill him when a horse kicks Jorg into the wizard and he is freed from the spell, killing Corion immediately.

Gorgoth is holding open a gate the Count fled through and Jorg bolts after. We learn here that Count Renar is actually King Olidan’s brother, meaning he is Jorg’s uncle, and had his own sister-in-law killed. Young Gog has some sort of flame magic and kills many soldiers, allowing Jorg to attack Renar.

Jorg is now writing a reflection of this account up in a tower, with Renar trapped in a dungeon to torture and Jorg king of the area. He plans his next moves and dares anyone to stop him. THE END.
