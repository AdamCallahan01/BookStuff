So Long, and Thanks for All the Fish
Douglas Adams
The Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy #4

So Long, and Thanks for All the Fish Summary

Arthur returns to Earth, after having heard it was somehow still in existence. He has hitchhiked a very long way and lands on a miserable, rainy day when most cars just ignore him. One of the drivers, Rob McKenna, particularly hates rain because it is always raining, but this is actually just because he is a rain god and the clouds love him.

Meanwhile, Ford Prefect is in the Old Pink Dog Bar in Han Dold City on a planet somewhere else. He offers to write an entry for the bar in the Guide to cover himself, then meets with a lady and complains to her about his wasted years on Earth. He goes to show her his entry in the Guide, and discovers, instead of being deleted when Earth was destroyed, it has everything he ever wrote about the planet. He is super curious and decides to hitchhike back to Earth, calling Arthur a few times along the way.

Arthur gets picked up by a brusque man named Russell with his unconscious, drugged sister, Fenny, in the backseat. Russell explains she is mad and was put under sedation for her own good. Arthur feels a strange attraction and connection to Fenny. He also learns most people believe the Vogon ships that showed up to destroy the Earth were hallucinations caused by the CIA. Then the ships just vanished.

Arthur returns to his house to find it not destroyed, and much the way he left it just with more dust and mold. He does find a gift, and opening it finds a unique, shiny fishbowl, with the words “So long and thanks” carved into it. He fills it with water and places the Babel fish from his ear into it. He also ignores several phone calls.

He goes into a trance-like dream state and senses this Earth is on top of another, destroyed one. He also feels a flaw, and thinks this must be Fenny. He puts his life back in order shockingly easily, it has only been 6 months on Earth but 8 years for Arthur. After failing to locate Fenny via the phone he heads to the local pub and tells everyone he was in California and drinks a lot.

Arthur is at a service station shop listening to Rob McKenna complain about rain, when Fenny walks in. Arthur talks to her and offers to drive her wherever she is going. He learns her name is Fenchurch and only her brother calls her Fenny, but they keep getting interrupted by an old lady running a raffle. Fenchurch writes her phone number on a raffle ticket for Arthur so he can call her, then gets on her train. Arthur ends up winning the raffle and they take the ticket with her phone number so he lost contact with her again.

In California there is a man named John Watson, who goes by Wonko the Sane. He will come up later.

Arthur ends up talking to Rob McKenna in the same shop again and tells him he should show his book telling how it always rains on him to someone. Arthur, who is very sad about losing Fenchurch, buys a computer and learns the dolphins vanished when the Earth wasn’t destroyed, which is very odd.

Arthur spends a while trying to calculate where the cave is that he lived in 2 million years in the past, using the stars and such. He thinks he finds it and goes to the spot, knocks on the door, and Fenchurch answers. She has to go out for the day but when she returns they go to Hyde park and hit it off immensely. They share stories of their past and Fenchurch does say there is something wrong with her, but she isn’t mad. She eventually shows Arthur his copy of the Hitchhiker's Guide which he lost, and asks if he can take her to see some of the stuff in it. 

They return to her place and Arthur eventually realizes Fenchurch’s feet don’t actually touch the ground. Arthur decides to show her he can fly, and teaches her how. Together they soar around and almost fall and die once. They eventually have sex on the wing of an airplane, and continue to fly around in love so much people begin to notice. The news is already in a tizzy over Rob McKenna, who showed scientists his notebook and now everyone knows he is in fact a rain god. There are also stories about the mysterious flying couple. They decide to get away for a while and go to visit John Watson (called Wonko the Sane), who claims to know what happened to the dolphins.

They get to his house, which is inside-out. He claims the rest of the Earth is in The Asylum, and he lives outside it. They talk and he shows he also received one of the fishbowl gifts, as did Fenchurch. His says “So long, and thanks for all the fish”. They learn that the dolphins prepared for Earth’s destruction by pulling in this one from another dimension and leaving, so the dolphins saved Earth.

Arthur plans to bring Fenchurch to God’s final message to his creation, which he was told about by Prak at the end of the last book. Ford reaches Arthur’s house finally about this point, and tells him about his adventures. The spaceship he hitchhiked on also landed in the middle of a city and released a giant robot asking to speak to a lizard, so the world is in a bit of chaos.

Ford manages to get them back on the spaceship before it can leave by pretending to be a scientist, and they all head off into the broader galaxy. Fenchurch and Arthur head to the planet of God’s Final Message to His Creation, and find it a challenging hike but it has been very commercialized and there are shops and rest stops. On the way they encounter Marvin, badly damaged and as pessimistic as ever. He complains he has lived multiple times the length of the universe due to all the time travel. They carry him to the end of the trail and see the message, which is “We apologize for the inconvenience”. Marvin says he feels good about it and dies.

There is a random last page talking about a man who designs great things, Blart Versenwald III, and managed to stop a war but there is no point to this. THE END.
