The Core
Peter V. Brett
The Demon Cycle #5

The Core Summary

Arlen, Jardir, Renna, Shanvah, and Shanjat still remain at the tower with the trapped mind demon prince that they call Alagai Ka. He has taken control of Shanjat’s body, stripping him of his own will. They plan to leave and descend into the core with him as their guide.

Hasik, the eunuch who killed Jayyan (Jardir’s eldest son), has taken Abban prisoner and castrated him in revenge. Every morning he breaks one of Abban’s bones to slowly torture him. They find groups of Krasians who fled after Jayyan’s failed attack on the city of Angiers, and Hasik attacks and dominates one, castrating all of the men he can so none are greater than him. They keep “chin” slaves to care for them, and Abban introduces Hasik to stuff forbidden by Everam, such as eating pig. As he continues to dominate more and more men and give in to more pleasures, he forsakes the fight against demons and cares only for himself. People begin calling him Eunuch Ka and he gathers a large force around him.

Leesha has returned to the Hollow as countess, and is making plans to continue helping people and expanding the great wards that keep the demons at bay. She gives birth to her “daughter”, Olive Paper. Olive has both male and female genitalia, but after consulting with the demon bone dice, Leesha and Amanvah decide to call Olive a girl to help keep her safe. They hold a funeral for Rojer, after which Regan and Elissa arrive from Miln. Arlen then reveals himself to this small group, including Gared and Wanda Cutter, and tells them how he and his group plan to go into the core and try to kill the demon queen. He warns them the next new moon / waning to expect major mind demon attacks.

Regan and Elissa are given the magic songs that keep demons away as well as the secret of Hora (demon bone) magic. They each craft themselves magic styluses they can use to draw wards in the air and power them. They also traveled with someone named Derik, who was in book 1.5 I believe and knows Arlen, as well as Keiran, the jongleur from book 1 who visited Tibbet’s Brook and brought Arlen back to Miln. As they begin their return journey to Miln they encounter more and more demon attacks, and the demons seem smarter. Keiran grows a spine and uses the fiddle magic to help keep people safe but it is a dangerous journey and many people die, although they do eventually reach Miln.

Inevera is struggling to keep the Krasian court united. Her son Asome and his allies killed a bunch of leaders of the clans and tried to instate their own power, using hora magic and attacking the night which is taboo in Krasian society. Ashia was almost killed by her brother, Asome’s lover Asukaji, but she paralyzes him and flees, helping Inevera who was also attacked. Inervera and her Sharum’Ting warriors are forced to be on the defensive, and Inervera’s father is killed in the struggle. She sends Ashia and Ashia’s infant son Kaji away, telling her to seek out Abban as the dice say he has an important role to play in the fight against the demons. 

Jardir is able to set things right again by briefly returning to court and beating his son into submission. Asome now listens to Inevera and she again begins planning how to best win the war. Amanvah and Sikvah return, pregnant with Rojer’s children. They realize they have to go to Everam’s Reservoir (Once known as docktown) as the demons will attack in force there. 

Arlen, Jardir, Renna, Shanvah, and Shanjat, escorting the demon prince, begin to head down into the core, which apparently is just a really long deep tunnel down? They pass by ruins and stuff marking when Kaji, Jardir’s ancestor, tried to march down and eradicate the demons 5,000 years ago. Things are complicated when they learn Renna is pregnant but she tells Arlen she is coming no matter what. The magic she uses causes strange things and accelerates her pregnancy greatly but they continue one, fighting demons and some other oddities along the way.

Briar Demaj, the hogroot loving kid from the last book (And a novella I think) helps Leesha look into her “warded children” who have gone feral and are living in the woods. They ink tattoo wards on his hands but he leaves after seeing them eat demon meat and heads away, eventually meeting up with Ashia and helping her on her journey. Hasik, with his eunuch forces, have taken the monastery near Docktown, but they need the forces there to help defend Docktown from demons. They battle a mimic on the way and Ashia has to protect her son Kaji, but they eventually make camp in a cave near the monastery. Abban is still being tortured by Hasik, who seems to fall even more into evil.

-- Start of Waning --

Leesha prepares for the defense of the Hollow on waning, but no attack comes. She sends messengers and they learn the Angiers has fallen, and a mind demon now resides there. She and Gared organize an army and march on the city.

Inevera struggles to hold Everam’s Reservoir, even after signing a truce with the people of Fort Lacton on the water. Sikvah dies in the attacks but they keep her body alive to save the baby.

Regan and Elissa arrived back in Miln, got involved in some pointless politics, then struggle to help the city survive. They both use magic extensively and Elissa loses some of the function of her legs to a demon attack, and Derik’s (the random guy from book 1.5) wife dies, so he is angry and in a custody battle for his child I guess. The demons over run the keep kill everyone and Regan is named the new Duke of Miln, but is struggling to keep his people alive.

Arlen, Jardir, and crew find an old temple of Kaji deep underground but otherwise just continue to kill random enemies (ghost dogs and mushrooms and magic eating worms) and near the core.

Hasik takes Abban down into the depths of the monastery and Abban sees Hasik has been serving a mind demon. Abban gets into a battle of wills with the mind demon and just at that moment Ashia, Kaji, and Briar sneak into the room. They are able to kill the mind demon and Hasik and free the people of the monastery, who go to help Inevera at the Reservoir.

Leesha arrives in Angiers and is forced to team up with Jessa, the weed gatherer who Leesha caught poisoning the duke in the last book. They plan a trap for the demons using the help of the demon dice and manage to blow up part of the keep and kill all the mind demons, freeing the city to keep fighting.

Big climax:
Arlen and Jardir got separated from Renna, Shanvah, and Shanjat. They see a weird caveman race of Krasians who have been living down here for thousands of years, and get called Everam, which makes Jardir question his own faith. The mind demon prince escapes them and we don’t see him the rest of the book. The two of them enter the Queen’s chamber, she is a huge bloated demon laying eggs constantly with a huge, powerful scorpion tail. She summons the remaining mind demons and they do battle.

Arlen and Jardir kill some but are just outnumbered. Jardir loses the spear of Kaji and Arlen gets stabbed by the queen’s stinger and pumped full of venom, which his magic can’t heal. Meanwhile, Renna, Shanvah, and Shanjat also snuck down and enter from a different path, with Renna sneakily grabbing the spear of Kaji and thrusting it into the demon queen’s eye. Jardir then jumps and shoves it in all the way, killing her. They think they have won but then 6 large eggs come out of the queen and 6 new queens are born, set on destroying these threats. They kill Shanvah and Shanjat easily, and the rest are forced on the defensive as Arlen slowly dies.

Arlen goes into a sort of spiritual magic state, invents new wards to kill queen demons, then sends his spirit out over the entire world. He uses the immense magic in the core to cast wards and kill all the demons fighting his friends, ending the combat in Miln, Angiers, Everam’s Reservoir, Tibbet’s Brook, and any other major city. He basically just Deus Ex Arlen’s every single fight happening anywhere, including killing the remaining demons threatening Renna and Jardir in the core. He then spreads his spirit too far and lets himself die.

Renna gives birth to her and Arlen’s child right after this, naming him Darin. Her and Jardir eventually make it back out of the core weeks later and return to their respective peoples. 

Fast forward a bit, and Leesha invites all of the leaders of the now free people to meet and sign a treaty together. Regan and Elissa come from Miln, the old duke of Angiers infant son, the leader of Lacton, Jardir, Inevera, Abban, Renna for some reason, and some others. They all sign pacts making them friends with their own territories. One tribe of the Krasian’s actually returned to their original desert home earlier in the book and they ignored them so I assume that is for the next series. Everyone is happy and friendly and they show all their kids, Olive Paper, Darin Bales, Rojer’s daughter and son, Kaji (Ashia’s son) all playing together happily. There is hope for the future, although some demons still remain. THE END.
