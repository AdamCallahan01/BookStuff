The Emerald Storm
Michael J. Sullivan
The Riyria Revelations #4

The Emerald Storm Summary

Merrick Marius has come to Ratibor (Where Arista still acts as mayor) with a crossbow and a magic-immune poison on his bolt. Arista is tiredly doing her best to keep the town going when the wizard Esrahaddon arrives unexpectedly, asking her for help. He needs Arista to perform a locating spell on Degan Gaunt (which he can’t do without hands). Arista refuses because of her remaining anger at him and herself for their roles in her father’s death. Esrahaddon leaves and when outside Marrick sneaks up and shoots him with the poisoned bolt. Esrahaddon summons many types of magic to defend himself but Merrick wears the amulet Esrahaddon made for the heir of Novron (Presumably taken from Degan Gaunt when he was captured) that makes the wearer immune to magic. Arista runs out but Merrick is long gone. Esrahaddon tells her a cryptic message about finding the heir, the horn of Gylindora, being buried in Percepliquis, something will happen at Wintertide, and that everyone is in danger, then he dies. Arista takes his magic cloak and sets off.

Hadrian and Royce are back at the Rose and Thorn tavern in Medford with Gwen. Royce just asked Gwen to marry him and everyone seems happy, but Alric summons them to the castle. He shows them a note his scouts intercepted from Merrick to the regents of the New Empire. It tells of a ship, the Emerald Storm, that has a mission which will secure the New Empire’s victory. Alric asks them to go figure out what is happening, and at first Royce plans to refuse but Gwen is very upset and tells him he must go or Hadrian will die, so they agree and take the mission.

Originally they are unable to get spots on the ship since they are obviously not experienced sailors, but they happen to run into Wyatt Deminthal, the man who hired them to steal the sword in the first book which started this whole series of adventures. He still owes them so he agrees to get them spots on the ship, since he is the helmsman. Hadrian becomes cook and Royce serves as a topman, running about the ropes easily due to his elvish blood. Things quickly become interesting on the ship, as no one knows what the mission is but there is a Seret Knight sentinel, Dovin Thranic, on board, as well as Bernie, an old member of the Black Diamond who was in the gang with Royce.

In Aquesta, Amilia is working to prepare for Empress Modina’s wedding to Regent Ethelred at Wintertide. She has not talked to Modina about it but Saldur ordered it must happen. Amilia talks to a maid named Ella who does not seem to fit in very well in the castle.

Wyatt appoints a young boy, Poe, to be Hadrian’s assistant knowing Poe will do all the hard parts and Hadrian won’t have many responsibilities. One of the first stormy nights, a man falls from the top sails and many people suspect Royce pushed him off. One of the people trying to keep actual order on the ship is Wesley, a young noble who is doing his best to be fair and honorable, despite bullying from some of the older men who want to frame Royce for the accident.

After rounding the coast of Delgos, the Emerald Storm spots some Dacca raiders and soon after finds itself surrounded and under assault. Wyatt is furious at the captain because he forces them to continue on to their destination instead of fleeing. Wyatt meets with Royce and Hadrian and tells them he has put together a small group, and he plans to steal one of the smaller Dacca ships when they are attacked and flee. Royce and Wyatt steal all the important papers from the captain's cabin and then flee to the ship. We end up with a small Dacca ship containing Wyatt, Royce, Hadrian, Wesley, Thranic, Poe, two men named Derning and Grady, and a few others. There is almost violence immediately, as Wyatt tells everyone Thranic set fire to the Emerald Storm before fleeing, despite there being many elven prisoners in the hold. Royce defuses the situation by saying Wesley is the ranking officer and letting him maintain order, so they have an uneasy peace and continue to Calis.

In Aquesta, Amilia is worried about Modina, since she seems to think she is dead and is seeing/experiencing other peoples lives? Meanwhile, the serving girl “Ella” is actually Princess Arista in disguise. She has been working in the castle to try and find Degan Gaunt. She has been sleeping in an alley with a kind refugee family, and one day their son falls and is nearly dead, so she uses her magic to heal him. This news quickly spreads and the Imperial guards come looking for the Witch of Melengar, and Arista turns herself in. On the way to the castle she is rescued by a mysterious masked figure she has been hiding from lately, who reveals himself to be her old bodyguard Hilfred. He wears a mask because he was even more horribly burned and scarred after the events in book 2, and was worried what Arista would think of him. He reveals he has always loved her and they stay together planning to flee to Melengar. In the chaos Arista loses Esrahaddon’s magic cloak she had taken.

Our group on the Dacca ship reaches the port of Dagastan in Calis, and Wyatt goes into town to figure out their arrangements. They are continuing to follow the captain's written orders since Hadrian and Royce still want to figure out what is going on with Merrick’s plan and Thranic wants to see the orders completed. The group heads into the jungle with a guide to find the warlord Esrandabon Gile, whom Hadrian says he has met before. He spent some years in Calis in his early mercenary days. One night as they travel, Royce brings his ex-gang member Bernie into the forest to talk while Hadrian talks to the odd-man out in their group, an elderly historian named Bulard. Thranic follows Royce into the trees and shoots him with a crossbow. Royce dodges a deadly hit and kills Thranic’s other companion but is still injured. Wesley again defuses the situation and they continue on, reaching a Tenkin village the next day.

The Tenkin are a strange people. They are human but have adopted many of the ways of the Ghazel (goblins). Thranic meets with the Oberdaza (wizardish person) of the village and asks for his help to prove Royce is an elf. They trick the village seer into doing a reading on Royce and she almost dies in the process, causing the Tenkin to almost kill their party, but one of the warriors who recognizes Hadrian kills the Oberdaza and tells everyone what had happened. Royce recovers and they continue on to find Esrandabon Gile.
In Aquesta, Arista leaves Hilfred a note then sneaks back onto castle grounds disguised as a maid, planning to do another location spell to find Degan Gaunt, but Modina spots her from her window and summons her up. Amilia walks in to find Arista and Thrace talking like friends. Thrace/Modina has agreed to help Arista find Degan Gaunt, the true Heir of Novron. Amilia is upset but does not want to anger Modina so agrees not to tell anyone about Arista. While in the kitchens one day Amilia sees some guards take a pot of soup to the North Tower, but when she enters there is no one there except a single Seret Knight standing guard, but the soup mysteriously disappears. She tells Modina and Arista about this and Arista begins planning how to free Gaunt, who is presumably hidden somewhere the tower,

Esrandabon imprisons their party upon arrival despite Thranic’s protests, since they did not bring any of the weapons or elven prisoners he was promised. Apparently the New Empire has been sending the elves to Calis so they can be fed to the Ghazel. Royce helps them break out and is about to kill Thranic when the old historian, Bulard, tells them to let his small group go with Thranic, since they have an important quest where they will likely die anyway. Royce reluctantly agrees and they begin fighting their way out before Esrandabon stops the fighting. He has recognized Hadrian and wants to eat with him. Hadrian agrees for him and his group to fight in the arena, and if they win they will be given a ship and allowed to leave. They have learned about a huge fleet of 50,000 Ghazel serving Esrandabon that plans to attack Delgos (one of the last nations opposed to the New Empire). Drumindor, the large dwarven towers that defend the port, are going to explode on the new moon and then the fleet will invade, and Hadrian is determined to stop that.

They fight 5 on 5 against a group of Ghazel in the arena. Wesley saves the day by killing their mage but dies in the process, and only Hadrian, Royce, and Wyatt survive the fight. They are given their ship and supplies and return to the port of Dagastan, grabbing the few remaining crew members and Wyatt leads Hadrian, Royce, Poe, Derning, and Grady back to Delgos to warn everyone.

At the castle in Aquesta, Hilfred sneaks into the castle, and Arista uses magic to make herself look just like Regent Saldur. They enter the tower and order the Seret Knight to take them to Gaunt. He uses a gem in his sword to open a secret door in the floor and they descend. There is some sort of magic inhibition here and Arista’s disguise goes away, so Hilfred kills the knight, although they worry someone may have heard. They rush through the hidden prison and find Gaunt in a cell, but don’t have a key, and on the way to find one are caught by the real Saldur and 5 Seret Knights. Hilfred dies trying to save Arista, and Saldur orders her imprisoned with Gaunt.

At Tur Del Fur (the port city in Delgos), Wyatt tells them no one will believe him about an invasion and Drumindor being sabotaged, so Hadrian and Royce decide to do it themselves. They climb the outside of the massive dwarven towers (which they did sometime in the past for another mission), and turn off the master gear switch. Drumindor is built to harness a volcano's power, and can spew lava on any invading ships, but they believe it has been sabotaged and will blow up when they vent the extra energy on the full moon. While searching for the whatever Merrick did, they are captured by guards who already know their names and take them to prison. While chained up Royce figures out that Merrick never sabotaged Drumindor, but he left notes and clues to make them think he did so they would turn it off, allowing the invading fleet to reach the port. They hear the enemy ships and soldiers arrive, but are rescued from their cell by Derning, one of their ship mates. Apparently him and Grady work for Cornelius De Lur, the richest man and de facto ruler of Delgos. They flee the city.

Merrick walks into the castle in Aquesta to report the destruction of Delgos to the regents. Earl Archibald Ballentyne is also there. He is in love with Modina and is furious that Ethelred is marrying her just to become emperor. We also learn they are planning for Modina to have an “Accident” shortly after the wedding. Merrick tells them he has fulfilled his duties by killing Esrahaddon and stopping Delgos. On his way out he picks up Esrahaddon’s cloak he bribed from the sheriff and returns to his house in the city. Once there he meets with Wyatt, and congratulates him on a job well done with leading Royce and Hadrian to do what Merrick wanted. He returns Wyatt’s daughter Allie who he had held hostage to force him to do this. Wyatt warns Merrick he should be afraid of Royce’s vengeance. THE END.
