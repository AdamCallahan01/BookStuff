Illusory Education
Peter J. Lee
Primal Wizardry #1

Illusory Education Summary

*Audiobook spelling*

Kolin (Kole) grew up in the underwater city of Ilondria. The entire world got flooded in a big battle with primordial beings a while back and his city was trapped in a dome of air under the ocean. Kole started out life as a magical prodigy, achieving his vault and bridge (steps in becoming a wizard) at a young age. However, Kole found out he is both a Primal, someone who can only access magic of a single “font” (basically element), and also a sorcerer, wizards who gain spells randomly when in danger. These two effects (mostly the Primal one) crippled his development until he was falling behind kids his own age in school.

Kole was being bullied by a kid named Corbin, and ended up punching him and fleeing, using the only sorcerer spell he knows, invisibility. His parents, who disappeared in a pocket realm years ago, were officially declared dead today so his possessions and home are being sold to clear his debts. His uncle tries to take care of him but Kole is set on being a wizard so grabs his mothers locket and his dads blasting wand and flees into the sewers. When he tries to leave the city by the conventional means he sees people searching for an invisible person, so he sneaks out in a barrel of whale poop that floats to the surface.

He gets picked up by a boat of the ship clans, to which his father belonged. He befriends the sailor Meetch and his brother, the Stormcaller Jouran. They help him claim castaway status and promise to take him to Edgewater, where he plans to attend a magic academy. While on the ship he finds a stowaway half-elf named Amara, also headed to the magic school, and helps her. They both make it to the Academy of the Dawn and get enrolled as students.

Kole almost gets a really good mentor right away, but gets rejected because he is set on becoming an adventurer to try and save his parents who he believes are still alive. Kole has a lot of Will (magic juice) with 40, but spells that cost most wizards 5 will to cast cost him the full 40 due to his primal nature. Because of this he spends a lot of time studying spell forms and mechanics to find his own versions that he can cast for less will.

Amara is really interested in runes and artifacts and joins the crafters college, while Kole begins the pre-adventuring track. He meets a part voidling girl named Zale (Azalea) who grew up on campus and knows a lot about it. They team up with her “cousin” a dwarf named Raken who also grew up here. Many of the other students shun and ignore Zale due to Voidlings being considered evil by many, and they being to shun Kole as well, but he sticks by his new friends.

He has a run-in with another student, Gray. After they fight off some goblin rats that appear in the academy, Kole reveals his wizardry struggles and Gray freaks out and says he is going to get people killed by being a defective wizard, so they hate each other. Gray’s group, consisting of himself, Harold, Esme, and Mouse, used to be friends with Zale but after learning about her voidling part they ignored her as well.

The group consisting of Kole, Zale, Raken, and a new demonkin classmate Doug, do well in their adventuring exam against a tribe of goblins. They all train and hang out with Amara in their free time, and Zale invites them, since all are primals, to a special study group set up by her mother and her uncle. Kole is living in secret in the school library due to a lack of money, and finds a secret room to stay in.

After a while of training and learning together, Kole finally learns that Zale’s mom is the mysterious chancellor of the academy, which explains a lot, and her “uncle” that is helping them study is actually Tal of Storms, a hero from the dragon wars. Kole has feelings for Zale but Harold apologizes and asks her out, making Kole jealous.

Kole meets a strange young man named Theral in his secret library room. Theral has a problem where he teleports somewhere random sometimes then returns here, but he says Kole can stay. He gives Kole some books and advice on how to learn more spells. They have another adventuring class challenge which puts Kole’s group in a time dilated dungeon for 2 months, so he makes great spell progress and learns to cast Thunderwave for only 20 will. They save the town from an ice elemental and earn a good score, but Kole is struggling in some other classes due to lack of time. 

The academy announces that some primal students seem to have disappeared, including Amara’s sister who she came here to search for. Odd things keep happening around Kole, like more monsters appearing in the academy and attacking him and Zale, but they hold them off. Overall Kole and his group make good progress but the first year is not over and Kole still needs to find a mentor to stay in the academy. In the epilogue Zale’s mother is about to set off on a quest to the elf kingdoms to retrieve something. THE END.
