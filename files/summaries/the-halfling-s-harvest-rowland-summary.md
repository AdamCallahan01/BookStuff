The Halfling's Harvest
S. L. Rowland
Tales of Aedrea #3

The Halfling’s Harvest Summary

Marigold Bramblefoot is the owner of the Dew Drop Inn in Willowbrook. She inherited ownership from her parents 10 years ago, and one of her biggest failings is that she has not won the yearly wine contest held at the harvest festival, which her father won frequently. Her biggest rival is Galvin Deeproot, who hired a druid 10 years ago and since then has made a ton of money and great wine.

While going into town one day, Marigold bumps into a gnome researcher visiting to learn more about the harvest festival, Poppy. Poppy ends up staying at the Dew Drop Inn and her and Marigold slowly grow closer as she continues to research. Also working at the inn are the elf Alara, her husband and the inn’s chef Locke, and the siblings Finn and Gurdy who just do whatever.

Truly not much happens. Marigold and Poppy pick apples, then Marigold falls out of a tree so they have to go into town to get it healed by a cleric. Marigold occasionally gets mad at Galvin, who is kind of a jerk but makes a lot of money. His druid, Tanzy, connects with Marigold and they eventually become friends despite Marigold being a sore loser. Marigold and Poppy bake pies and drink wine together. They drink wine probably every chapter.

A wizard named Aldrich shows up with his little stick golem Splinter. He is in charge of the firework show at the upcoming harvest festival but starts to become friends with Marigold in the meantime. Alara and Marigold begin noticing strange slime trails and some missing grapes, but as they investigate they can’t figure it out. Marigold is stressing over which wine to enter in the upcoming festival, but Poppy calms her down and helps her read through her father’s old journals for inspiration. Marigold finally settles on a red wine, but then a few days before the festival, she goes down into the cellar and finds a massive jelly slime monster drinking all her wine. Aldrich explains it is his pet he tried to keep secret but it destroyed almost all the red wine, so Marigold is distraught. Poppy kisses her and she calms down and they start actually dating.

Marigold instead enters into the contest a mixed blend of all 3 of her wines, after getting inspiration from her fathers journals. The day of the harvest festival Locke wins the pie baking contest, and Galvin is being insufferable. Marigold has multiple panic attacks and almost passes out but eventually wins the wine contest. Galvin freaks out and claims he should have won, so Tanzy traps him in vines and quits working for him. Everyone is happy since winning this contest is the only thing they care about. Poppy has to return home since her research is done which is sad.

A while later Marigold’s parents come to visit home and try the winning win, and Poppy moves back and opens a local historical society so she can be with Marigold. THE END.
