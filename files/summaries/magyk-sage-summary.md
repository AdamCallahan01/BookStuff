Magyk
Angie Sage
Septimus Heap #1

Magyk Summary

Silas Heap is returning to the castle after gathering some herbs for his newly born seventh son, Septimus Heap. As he returns he finds another small infant wrapped up and left in the snow. No one is about so he takes the child home with him. After passing the irascible gatekeeper Gringe, he is stopped by the ExtraOrdinary wizard, Marcia Overstrand, who tells him to pretend the child he carries is his own. As he reaches his small home in the Ramblings (an offshoot of the greater castle city complex) the midwife rushes past him carrying a baby crying that Septimus is dead. Silas goes in to comfort his wife Sarah and their 6 other sons, and shows her the new baby girl, who they claim as their own.

10 years later, the baby girl, named Jenna, is still living with the Heaps. The day she was born, the old ExtraOrdinary wizard, Alther Mella, and the Queen were killed. The Supreme Custodian has taken over ruling the castle since and has instituted harsh new policies against wizardry. Alther is a ghost and has continued to hang around the castle. On Jenna’s tenth birthday, he overheads when the Supreme Custodian is told the princess still lives (Jenna) and where she is hiding, and he sends an assassin to kill her. Alther rushes to warn Marcia, who then goes to see the Heap’s. She explains the situation to them, and Jenna agrees to go with Marcia to the Wizard Tower while the rest of the family flees to stay with Galen the Physik in the forest.

Outside the wizard tower Marcia and Jenna rescue a scrawny boy from the Young Army who has fallen in the snow, and bring him up with them to warm him up. At the top of the tower they warm the boy up, then Silas Heap arrives, with his youngest son Nicko and their wolfhound Maxie. Shortly after this, the assassin shows up with her gun, but Marcia protects them using magic. They are forced to flee through the garbage shoot that runs under the castle. They land in a rubbish heap and head to Sally Mullin’s tea shop. Sally is a friend of Sarah Heap and helps them flee on a ship, the Muriel.

The Hunter has been set on their trail, and tracks them to Sally’s shop. After interrogating her and learning from a customer they fled on a boat downriver, he sets fire to Sally’s shop, and Sally only escapes because Marcia left her a magic artifact. Sally runs to hide with Sarah and the Heap boys at Galen’s in the forest. The Muriel and her crew are headed to Silas’s Aunt Zelda, the Keeper of the Marram Marshes. The Hunter, accompanied by a wizard apprentice, chases them down but they manage to evade him in a magyk fog and find the ghost of Alther in the marshes. Alther tells them his old master, the necromancer DomDaniel, has returned and claimed the title of ExtraOrdinary wizard of the castle. Silas uses his magyk to convert Muriel into two canoes and, with the help of friendly boggart, they make their way to Aunt Zelda’s cottage.

When the hunter and the apprentice return to DomDaniel he is furious that they failed and still set on capturing the princess (Jenna).

At Aunt Zelda’s cottage not much happens at first. The young army boy, Boy 412, slowly gets to know Jenna and Nicko but does not speak. They learn he has very powerful magyk when Marcia is having them prepare some shield bugs as defense against attackers. While exploring Boy 412 falls down a hole and finds strange tunnels, and a magic ring which he hides in his hat. The tunnels lead back up to the cottage and he pretends nothing happened. They receive a message rat from Sarah, who tells them everything is ok with her and the boys. Silas returns to the Castle with the message rat in order to retrieve Marcia’s KeepSafe, the magic amulet she gave Sally.

Silas finds his family and learns that Simon, his eldest son, returned to the castle since he had secretly planned to marry Lucy Gringe, daughter of the gatekeeper. Silas is guided by Alther to a secret tavern in the castle and learns Simon has been captured by the Supreme Custodian, and together they try to figure out how to free him. Meanwhile, Stanley the message rat returned to the message rat offices and found new, mean rats in charge.  They made him take a message to Marcia pretending to be from Silas but actually from the supreme custodian, and he is forced to accept. On the way he gets captured by Mad Jack and kept in a cage.

Soon the Big Freeze arrives, bringing a ton of snow and ice and no one can really travel. Simon reveals to the Supreme Custodian where Jenna is at Aunt Zelda’s cottage but no one can do anything about it yet. Stanley manages to escape his cage by not eating for weeks and squeezing between the bars, making it to Aunt Zelda’s cottage. He delivers his message, telling Marcia to meet Silas at midnight, which she believes. That night she teleports back to the castle and is captured, with DomDaniel taking the Akhu Amulet that gives the ExtraOrdinary wizard more power.

Shortly after the snow and ice begin thawing, DomDaniel sends the hunter and his apprentice back after Jenna. They shoot the boggart on the way and he barely lives. The shield bugs chase off the apprentice and Aunt Zelda manages to freeze the hunter, so they are safe. Nicko captures the apprentice and he tells them he is Septimus Heap, and some scrying seems to confirm that. They use a memory spell to make the Hunter forget who he was and he leaves to become a circus clown.

Simon escapes on his own and finds Silas, and together they flee to meet with Sarah and the family. DomDaniel sets out on his Darke magyk boat, the Vengeance. The apprentice manages to slip away from Aunt Zelda’s cottage and flees in a canoe. Jenna, Nicko, and Boy 412 chase after him, but he reaches the Vengeance docked outside the marsh. Alther’s ghost tells them Marcia is onboard, so they use invisibility spells and sneak on. Boy 412 gives Marcia the magic ring he found earlier but then they are discovered and forced to flee. DomDaniel is enraged and summons a massive storm, flooding the marsh and allowing them to sail straight to Aunt Zelda’s cottage.

Back at the cottage, Jenna, Nicko, and Boy 412 flee into the basement tunnels and discover a majestic dragon boat, the dragon boat of Hotep-Ra, the first ExtraOrdinary Wizard. Because Jenna is technically the queen the dragon likes her, and they use Boy 412’s magic to get it going and they ride the waves, and eventually fly towards the Vengeance. Marcia uses the magic ring to escape her cell, and she steals the Akhu Amulet back from DomDaniel, and escapes with the kids on the dragon boat.

Back at the cottage, Marcia and Zelda send a ton of Brownies to attack the Vengeance, which sinks. Shortly after they find the apprentice crawling up to them, and take pity on him, but when Marcia arrives she kills the “boy” telling them it was DomDaniel in disguise. She takes them to the nearly dead actual apprentice, but Zelda is able to save him. Silas, Sarah, and the Heap boys arrive shortly after. Boy 412 agrees to be Marcia’s apprentice, which upsets Simon since he has always wanted to be ExtraOrdinary wizard. They have a party to celebrate and Zelda scries to reveal that Boy 412 is actually Septimus Heap, and DomDaniel got his apprentice in a mix up. THE END.
