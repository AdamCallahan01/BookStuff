Mostly Harmless
Douglas Adams
The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy #5

Mostly Harmless Summary

A large Grebulon ship is travelling through the galaxy when something goes wrong with all of its systems, and when the crew gets woken up to handle the issue their minds, which had been stored in the system, are not restored so they have no idea what to do. They only have a garbled message saying to monitor so they set up on Pluto, which they call Rupert, and monitor the Earth.

Tricia McMillan, in some other dimension/reality, where she did not go to space with Zaphod Beeblebrox due to her going to her bag and him leaving because she took too long, is in New York working as a television reporter. She interviews a lady about her astrology book, then fails an audition for a major job upgrade, and is feeling very dejected. She heads home, but is shocked to see an alien spaceship land on her lawn. It is the Grebulons, who ask for her help recalculating their systems so they can get accurate horoscopes. She sees this as her big break and brings a camera to film everything and leaves with them.

Ford is breaking into the headquarters of the Guide, which is apparently his normal way to enter, however this time something is slightly off. He hijacks a small robot he calls Colin who emits happiness signals making all the other robots let them by. He reaches the editor-in-chief’s office, and instead of finding his old boss he finds Vann Harl, who works for InfiniDem Enterprises, who have in turn purchased the Hitchhiker’s Guide. Harl tries to demote Ford to restaurant critic so Ford knocks him out and takes his ID and credit card. Ford then heads down to the main computer terminal, and uses Harl’s ID to get authorization and sets himself up to get automatic expense approval for anything he wants basically, then hides his work, discovering someone else has already hidden something fairly large in the system. He goes to return the ID but finds a bunch of robots with rocket launchers. He manages to slip the ID back into Harl’s pocket but then is forced to flee out a broken window after a robot fires a rocket through it. While falling he uses his trusty towel to lasso Colin the happy robot and slow his fall, but then rocket robots appear on the ground. Ford kicks off a shoe which the robots shoot and the rocket blast kills the robots. Ford grabs onto a window ledge and jimmies the lock to enter the 13 floor, the R and D department. Here he finds the new version of the guide Harl told him they were working on, a single guide able to go to all dimensions thus be sold infinite times for infinite profit. Ford realizes the Vogons are behind this and sends the new guide to himself in the care of Arthur Dent to keep safe, then jumps out the window again when robots show up.

Arthur was traveling with Fenchurch through hyperspace one day when she just disappeared, and when he tried to complain they laughed at him saying people from plural sectors should know hyperspace travel is risky. He has spent years travelling to places Earth should be and finding similar but now right planets, such as the dreary WhatNow. He goes to a planet of oracles and seers to see if they know what he should do or look for, but just finds crazy smelly people. He learns that Trillian, now going by Trillian Astra in whatever dimension he is in, is a time-traveling reporter, making people who predict the future fairly useless. Arthur ends up just flying around in spaceships all the time, donating his DNA to afford money, hoping to either find Earth or disappear like Fenchurch. Eventually a ship of his crashes and he is the only survivor.

A spaceship lands outside a village on the planet of Lamuella. This is an unusual event, and most of the villagers go out to see what is happening. The sandwich maker is unbothered, because he is happy and loves his job and life. Two people exit the spaceship, ignoring the village elder Old Thrasbarg, and go to the sandwich maker, who is Arthur Dent. One of the people is Trillian (Astra), who introduces him to Random, their daughter. Trillian used the DNA Arthur donated but now is leaving the girl with him while she goes to cover a war.

This greatly disrupts Arthur’s briefly happy life, as Random is a very distressed child who causes a lot of issues, but Arthur tries his best. One day a package arrives from Ford, and Arthur does not want to open it. Random ends up stealing it and flees to the wreckage of the spaceship Arthur crashed on this planet in to open it. Arthur chases her but is too slow. Random opens it and the new guide can fly and talk and see the future and all possibilities. It prearranges for a spaceship to land here, which Random uses to leave. Arthur arrives right as she takes off and finds Ford Prefect lying on the ground, Random threw a rock at his head and stole the ship. Ford explains to Arthur how dangerous that guide is since it can manipulate time and such, and they need to figure out a way to save the universe again.

There are animals called Perfectly Normal Beasts, which appear and stampede then disappear. Ford and Arthur hop on one and when it disappears jump off to find themselves on a different planet. There is a bar with spaceships outside so Ford goes to buy one using his company card he stole earlier. They know Random is going to Earth so head there, since they seem to have ended up in a dimension where it exists. They arrive on Earth to find Random causing chaos, and berating Tricia McMillan who she thinks is her mother.

The Tricia that went with the Grebulons discovered they are quite boring and all her footage looks fake so is worth nothing. She goes to cover a UFO landing only to be harassed by Random immediately. Arthur and Ford chase Random and Tricia to a club, hoping to retrieve the new Guide and stop it. When they get there they also find Trillian Astra, who worries this world is about to end due to time shenanigans. Arthur is not worried since he met Agrajag in book 3 who said that Arthur got him killed on Stavro Mueller Beta which Arthur has never visited so knows he can’t die. Suddenly Random accidentally shoots the man behind Arthur, and Arthur sees that the club they are in is called Stavro Mueller Beta. Arthur accepts his own death has come.

A Vogon (Prostetnic Jeltz) has been watching Earth with his own copy of the new guide. He watches as something happens to the space-time continuum and Earth and all possible Earth’s collapse and are destroyed, along with the new version of the Guide. THE END.
